# BoxSpec fields in plain words

> Generated by `skills/square-hole-launch/scripts/build-references.mjs` from `packages/box-spec/box-spec.v1.json`, `add-ons.v1.json` and
> `plain-words.v1.json`: do not edit (run `pnpm launch-skill:write`). For what is offered at this moment, run
> `node scripts/check.mjs --menu` from the skill's folder (or `pnpm box-spec:check --menu` in the folder it ships in): it
> reads the same sources at run time. "ref." names the decision rows and slices behind an entry: never quote them.

Every new box uses context `v2` (version 2): native ETH (the one raise: 4.206980085 ETH).

How to write values in a draft (details in [spec-json.md](spec-json.md)): choices by name (`"TO_CREATOR"`),
amounts as `{ "decimal": "0.05" }` in whole units of the pricing asset, counts as plain numbers, the opening time as
`0` (Now) or a UTC time as text, `"2026-10-06T18:00:00Z"`, and
`"FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW"` for what the launch flow fills in (art fingerprints, the connected wallet). Never ask for a key.

## 1. Basics

### 1.1 Pricing asset · `q`

The asset buyers pay with: the token is priced in it, the raise is counted in it and buyers' fees are paid in it.

- **Set by:** the creator, from the version's curated list.
- **Allowed now:** "ETH" (native ETH) (others are planned for later).
- **Default:** native ETH.
- **Tell the creator:** Today only ETH is listed: buyers pay in ETH, the raise and the buyers' fees are in ETH, and there is no dollar price. USDC, FIT, other boxes' tokens and USDY (tokenized T-bills) are planned for later.
- **The checker accepts:** a listed asset's label (e.g. "ETH") or its address (0x000...000 is native ETH).
- **Planned:** USDC: a proposal for later; its ETH-equivalent raise rule is open (S12) (ref. F44 P3).
- **Planned:** FIT: once FIT trades (it is box 0's token); then the buyers' side could burn it (ref. slice S12).
- **Planned:** another box's token: later, only a graduated box's token that Square Hole lists (ref. slice S12; F73 example 9).
- **Planned:** USDY (tokenized T-bills): later, after a legal review (ref. F61 TB1).

### 1.2 Token name · `tokenName`

The full name of the box's token (1,000,000,000 are made, the same for every box).

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** text, 1 to 32 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \).
- **Default:** the collection name.
- **Tell the creator:** Fixed forever once created. Printable ASCII only: no emoji, accents, curly quotes or long dashes, and no " or \.

### 1.3 Ticker · `tokenSymbol`

The token's short symbol, e.g. GUZZ.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** text, 1 to 11 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \).
- **Default:** the collection symbol.
- **Tell the creator:** Up to 11 characters, fixed forever, upper case by habit. Write it without a leading $: sites add the $ themselves. Another box may use the same ticker; a unique one avoids confusion.

### 1.4 Logo · `coverSha256`

The fingerprint (sha256) of the box's logo: the token's picture and the box card. Stored for good. A new collection's own picture is the collection cover.

- **Set by:** the launch flow, from the uploaded image (it re-encodes the image first).
- **Allowed now:** "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow fills it in).
- **Default:** FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW.
- **Tell the creator:** Ask whether they have a square logo: PNG, JPEG or WebP, 256 to 4,096 pixels a side, up to 10 MiB as uploaded; the launch flow shrinks it to at most 1,024 pixels and 1 MiB. Fixed forever. Write "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" in a draft: a hash of the original file would not match.

### 1.5 Description · `description`

A short text about the box. Its first sentence is the box's one line on the site; it also goes into the collection's on-chain metadata.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** text, 0 to 1,024 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \).
- **Default:** empty.
- **Tell the creator:** One or two plain sentences, up to 1,024 characters, the same ASCII rule as the name. Fixed forever.

## 2. Contents

### 2.1 Contents · `boxType`

What the box holds besides its token.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** "NEW_COLLECTION" A new collection | "TOKEN_ONLY" Just the token. Not offered: "EXISTING" (later, with checks that the collection is the real one).
- **Default:** none: the creator picks one (the launch flow pre-selects neither).
- **Tell the creator:** Just the token (no NFTs and no vault; the logo is the token's picture) or a new NFT collection whose items sit in the box's vault and back the token.

| Value | Plain label | What it means | Offered now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `"NEW_COLLECTION"` | A new collection | A new NFT collection made for the box; its items sit in the box's vault and back the token. | yes |
| `"EXISTING"` | Existing NFTs | NFTs that already exist, deposited into the box. | not yet: later, with checks that the collection is the real one (ref. slice S15; F71 (1)) |
| `"TOKEN_ONLY"` | Just the token | A plain token launch: no NFTs and no vault; the logo is the token's picture. | yes |

### 2.2 Collection name · `collectionName`

The NFT collection's name, shown on marketplaces.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** text, 1 to 32 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \).
- **Default:** the token name.
- **Tell the creator:** Usually the same as the token name; only ask if they want them different. Empty for a token-only box.

### 2.3 Collection symbol · `collectionSymbol`

The NFT collection's symbol.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** text, 1 to 11 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \).
- **Default:** the ticker.
- **Tell the creator:** Usually the same as the ticker. Empty for a token-only box.

### 2.4 Collection cover · `collectionCoverSha256`

The fingerprint (sha256) of the collection's own picture: its image on marketplaces (contractURI) and, with open art, the picture of every item not yet revealed. Stored for good. Zero for a box with no collection.

- **Set by:** the launch flow, from its own upload (a separate picture from the logo).
- **Allowed now:** "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow fills it in).
- **Default:** FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW for a new collection, zero for just the token.
- **Tell the creator:** A new collection needs its own cover picture, uploaded separately from the logo. With open art it is also every item's picture until that item is revealed (proposed; this may change to the logo). Write "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" in a draft; zero ("0x0") when the box has no collection.

### 2.5 Items · `n`

How many NFTs the collection holds, numbered 1 to n.

- **Set by:** the launch flow counts it from the art files.
- **Allowed now:** 10 to 10,000.
- **Default:** the number of art files.
- **Tell the creator:** 10 to 10,000 items, one picture each, named by its number (1.png to n.png, or .jpg, .webp; 0007.png counts as 7; no 0 and no gaps). PNG, JPEG or WebP only (no TIFF, GIF or SVG), each up to 1 MiB and 16 to 4,096 pixels a side; pictures are stored as uploaded. The launch flow counts n from the files, so a draft's n is a plan before the art exists. A token-only box writes 0.

### 2.6 Art shown · `artMode`

Whether every item's art is public from the start (open) or hidden until each item is drawn (hidden, with tickets during the curve).

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** "OPEN" Open art | "HIDDEN" Hidden until drawn.
- **Default:** OPEN.
- **Tell the creator:** Open is simpler. Hidden needs the placeholders add-on and a reveal file the creator must keep safe; the trait names and values are public from creation either way. A token-only box writes 0 (no art).

| Value | Plain label | What it means | Offered now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `"OPEN"` | Open art | Every item's art and traits are public from the start. | yes |
| `"HIDDEN"` | Hidden until drawn | Buyers get tickets during the curve; items are revealed one by one by random draws. Needs the placeholders add-on and a reveal file the creator keeps safe. | yes |

### 2.7 Trait table · `metadataTable`

The trait names and values (and the description) stored on chain.

- **Set by:** the launch flow computes it from the traits file (optional).
- **Allowed now:** "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow fills it in).
- **Default:** FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW.
- **Tell the creator:** Optional: no traits is fine (write "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW", or { "formatV1": { "traitTypes": [] } }). The launch flow reads a traits file: CSV with a first column "id" and one column per trait (an empty cell means none), JSON, or a folder of <id>.json metadata files (only "attributes" is read). At most 16 trait types with up to 255 values each, the same ASCII rule as names. A formatV1 table in a draft is a preview: the table stored is the one the launch flow builds from the file. It is public from creation, also with hidden art. A token-only box writes "0x" (no table).

### 2.8 Art commitment · `revealRoot`

A fingerprint of every item's art, traits and secret salt, so nobody can swap the art later.

- **Set by:** the launch flow computes it from the art.
- **Allowed now:** "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow fills it in).
- **Default:** FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW.
- **Tell the creator:** Never ask for it. Write "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" in a draft ("0x0" for a token-only box).

### 2.9 Reveal list · `revealListId`

Open art: the id of the public list of every item's art, stored at creation. Hidden art: zero (the list stays secret in the reveal file).

- **Set by:** the launch flow.
- **Allowed now:** "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow fills it in).
- **Default:** FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW for open art, zero for hidden art and for a token-only box.
- **Tell the creator:** Never ask for it.

### 2.10 Generator hash · `generatorHash`

An optional fingerprint of the program that generated the art.

- **Set by:** the creator (optional).
- **Allowed now:** zero (none).
- **Default:** zero (none).
- **Tell the creator:** Leave it zero: the launch flow has no field for it, so a draft with a generator hash cannot be entered there yet.
- **The launch flow:** always writes zero; `"FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW"` in a draft means that zero.

### 2.11 Collection owner · `marketplaceOwner`

The address marketplaces treat as the collection's owner (it can edit the collection page there). No box contract reads it.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** a public 0x address, or "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the wallet connected at launch, or the address pasted there).
- **Default:** the wallet connected in the launch flow (FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW); zero for a token-only box.
- **Tell the creator:** In the launch flow one public 0x address is the collection owner and the fee payout address: the wallet the creator connects, unless they paste another address (e.g. a team Safe) under Your address on the Overview page. Royalties to the creator are paid to that same fee payout address, through the box's royalty router (marketplaces see the router). A token-only box has no collection owner: write zero. Never a private key or seed phrase.

## 3. Box kind

### 3.1 Buyers' fees · `qStream`

What happens to the box's 10/15 share of the 1% fee that buyers pay (in the pricing asset, ETH today). The other 5/15 is fixed: 3/15 to FIT buy-and-burn, 2/15 into the box's own pool.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** "BUYBACK_BURN_T" Buy back and burn the token | "ALL_TO_FIT" Burn FIT | "BURN_Q" Burn the pricing asset (only when the pricing asset is FIT or a listed box token (none is listed yet); not on Sepolia yet: needs a Q with a real burn (S12)) | "TO_CREATOR" To the creator, in ETH | "CREATOR_IN_T" To the creator, in the token (not on Sepolia yet: needs the swap module). Not offered: "PAY_HOLDERS_Q" (Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review; --network demo), "HOLDERS_IN_T" (Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review; --network demo).
- **Default:** BUYBACK_BURN_T.
- **Tell the creator:** This is the 'kind of box', chosen for each fee side on its own: to the creator (in ETH, or converted into the token) or burn. Holder payouts: Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once built, on mainnet only after the legal review.

| Value | Plain label | What it means | Offered now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `"BUYBACK_BURN_T"` | Buy back and burn the token | The box buys its own token with the ETH fees and burns it: less supply for holders. | yes |
| `"ALL_TO_FIT"` | Burn FIT | The share joins FIT's buy-and-burn (FIT is the platform token, box 0's token). | yes |
| `"BURN_Q"` | Burn the pricing asset | Burns the pricing asset itself; only when it can really be burned (FIT or a listed box token). | yes, only when the pricing asset is FIT or a listed box token (none is listed yet) |
| `"PAY_HOLDERS_Q"` | Pay holders in ETH | Token holders collect the share in ETH. | not yet: Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review (ref. slice S14; F42; F74 U1) |
| `"TO_CREATOR"` | To the creator, in ETH | The creator's payout address (a person's wallet or a team Safe) collects the share in ETH, paid as collected. During the curve it is held until graduation and goes into the refund range if the box fails. | yes |
| `"CREATOR_IN_T"` | To the creator, in the token | The ETH fees buy the token in small limited steps after graduation and the creator collects that. | yes |
| `"HOLDERS_IN_T"` | Pay holders in the token | The ETH fees buy the token (limited steps) for token holders. | not yet: Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review (ref. F70; slice S14; F74 U1) |

### 3.2 Sellers' fees · `tStream`

What happens to the box's 10/15 share of the 1% fee that sellers pay (in the box's own token).

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** "BURN_T" Burn the token | "SELL_FOR_Q" Burn FIT (sells the token for ETH) (not on Sepolia yet: needs the swap module) | "PER_ITEM_STREAM" Stream to each NFT (box 0 (the Misfits) only, with its per-item-stream add-on; there it streams 13/15 of sellers' fees and 2/15 goes into the pool) | "TO_CREATOR" To the creator, in the token | "CREATOR_IN_Q" To the creator, in ETH (not on Sepolia yet: needs the swap module). Not offered: "PAY_HOLDERS_T" (Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review; --network demo), "HOLDERS_IN_Q" (Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review; --network demo).
- **Default:** BURN_T.
- **Tell the creator:** Paid as collected (tokens stay tokens) is the default; anything that sells the token for the pricing asset puts sell pressure on it and shows a warning.
- **The launch flow:** offers each side on its own (any pair the check accepts).

| Value | Plain label | What it means | Offered now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `"BURN_T"` | Burn the token | Sellers' fee tokens are burned: less supply for holders. | yes |
| `"PAY_HOLDERS_T"` | Pay holders in the token | Token holders collect the share in the box's token. | not yet: Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review (ref. slice S14; F42; F74 U1) |
| `"SELL_FOR_Q"` | Burn FIT (sells the token for ETH) | The fee tokens are sold for ETH after graduation in limited steps; that ETH joins FIT's share or is burned. This is sell pressure on the token: buyers see a warning. | yes |
| `"PER_ITEM_STREAM"` | Stream to each NFT | The share streams to every item in the collection. | yes, box 0 (the Misfits) only, with its per-item-stream add-on; there it streams 13/15 of sellers' fees and 2/15 goes into the pool |
| `"TO_CREATOR"` | To the creator, in the token | The creator's payout address collects the share in the token, paid as collected (nothing is sold for them; selling it later is visible on chain). During the curve it is held until graduation and goes into the refund range if the box fails. | yes |
| `"CREATOR_IN_Q"` | To the creator, in ETH | The box sells the fee tokens for ETH in small limited steps after graduation and the creator collects ETH. Sell pressure: buyers see a warning. | yes |
| `"HOLDERS_IN_Q"` | Pay holders in ETH | The fee tokens are sold for ETH (limited steps) for token holders. Sell pressure: buyers see a warning. | not yet: Demo only for now, marked pending legal review; on testnet once holder payouts are built, on mainnet only after the legal review (ref. F70; slice S14; F74 U1) |

### 3.3 Fee payout address · `creatorFeeRecipient`

Where the creator's fee share and creator royalties are credited (as claims the creator collects). Must be zero when neither goes to the creator.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** a public 0x address, or "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the wallet connected at launch, or the address pasted there).
- **Default:** the owner address with a creator option, else zero.
- **Tell the creator:** The launch flow sets it to the owner address when a fee side or the royalties go to the creator (one payout wallet per box; teams use a Safe). Only its current holder can hand it over.

## 4. Add-ons (set on Contents and Box kind)

### 4.1 Add-ons · `addOns`

Optional extras from the add-on catalog, each with its own settings, listed by ascending id.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Default:** none.
- **Tell the creator:** Hidden art needs the placeholders add-on (write its params as "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" for the launch flow's own texts and picture); royalties are off unless chosen.

### Hidden art (placeholders) · `"placeholders"` (id 1)

Tickets during the curve; hidden items revealed by random draws. Added automatically with hidden art.

- **Available in:** version 1 and 2.
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`.
- **Needs:** Placeholders open into hidden items, so they need hidden art; an open-art box has nothing hidden to draw.
- **Tell the creator:** The launch flow sets the ticket name ('<Name> ticket'), its symbol ('<TICKER>-T') and a neutral hidden picture; there the creator can change only the ticket text and the hidden item text. Write "params": "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" for all of its defaults, or only the two texts (a setting left out, or written "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW", takes the launch flow's value). For buyers, a draw costs one item's price in the box's token plus 1%, and the first draw of each randomness round also pays for posting that round (about 0.0003 ETH at 0.5 gwei; any ETH sent beyond what the round still needs comes back at once), which usually goes back to the drawer's own page.

What the launch flow writes itself (the checker uses these for `"FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW"`; the creator can change only
`placeholderDescription` and `hiddenDescription` there):

- `placeholderName`: the collection name cut to 25 characters, then "ticket".
- `placeholderSymbol`: the collection symbol cut to 9 characters, then "-T".
- `placeholderDescription`: "Opens into one item once its number is drawn."
- `hiddenDescription`: "This item is not revealed yet."
- `hiddenSvg` (the neutral picture, 274 characters):

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 64 64"><rect width="64" height="64" fill="#111b29"/><path d="M14 26 32 18l18 8-18 8z" fill="#30415d"/><path d="M14 26v14l18 8 18-8V26M32 34v14" fill="none" stroke="#a78bfa" stroke-width="2" stroke-linejoin="round"/></svg>
```

| Setting | What | Allowed | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `placeholderName` (Ticket name) | The ticket's name. | text, 1 to 32 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \) | '<Name> ticket' (the collection name cut to 25 characters) |
| `placeholderSymbol` (Ticket symbol) | The ticket's symbol. | text, 1 to 11 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \) | '<TICKER>-T' (the collection symbol cut to 9 characters) |
| `placeholderDescription` (Ticket text) | The ticket's description. | text, 0 to 1,024 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \) | 'Opens into one item once its number is drawn.' |
| `hiddenDescription` (Hidden item text) | What a not-yet-revealed item says. | text, 0 to 1,024 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \) | 'This item is not revealed yet.' |
| `hiddenSvg` (Hidden picture) | The picture of tickets and hidden items: plain SVG text on one line (no line breaks or tabs), up to 4,096 characters. | text, 1 to 4,096 characters (one line of printable ASCII; quotes are fine (written \" in JSON)) | the launch flow's neutral picture (it takes no upload yet) |

### Stream to each NFT · `"per-item-stream"` (id 2)

The sellers' share streams to every item in the collection.

- **Available in:** version 1 only (not for new boxes).
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`.
- **Needs:** The per-item stream is fed by the "Stream to each NFT" sellers' fee kind.
- **Tell the creator:** Box 0 (the Misfits) only for now: never offer it.

### Special series · `"edition-specials"` (id 3)

A named series of special items (e.g. 3 'Round Pegs'), shown as a count; which items they are stays secret for hidden art.

- **Available in:** version 1 and 2; may appear more than once.
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`.
- **Tell the creator:** Optional. The spec stores only the series' name and how many items belong to it: mark those items with a trait in the traits file (e.g. Edition: Golden) so buyers can find them. The launch flow takes up to 4 series (the spec allows 8 add-ons in total); the count is at most the number of items and is not checked against the files.

| Setting | What | Allowed | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `label` (Series name) | The series' name as the site shows it, 1-32 characters. | text, 1 to 32 characters (printable ASCII, no " or \) | none |
| `count` (How many) | How many items belong to the series. | 1 to n | none |

### Royalties · `"royalties"` (id 4)

A royalty rate on NFT resales that marketplaces may pay (unenforced, ERC-2981) and where it goes.

- **Available in:** version 1 and 2.
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`.
- **Needs:** Box 0 keeps its royalties inside its fee handling or pool: it cannot send them to a creator.
- **Tell the creator:** Off by default. Expect little: most marketplaces treat royalties as optional. Presets only: 0, 1, 2.5 or 5%. With the fees, a box whose fees go to the creator passes 10/15 of them on after graduation; to the creator, the box collects them and credits the fee payout address after graduation (one payout wallet per box).

| Setting | What | Allowed | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bps` (Rate) | The rate in basis points: 100 = 1%, 250 = 2.5%, 500 = 5% (0 = off; then leave the add-on out). | 0 \| 100 \| 250 \| 500 | off |
| `destination` (Goes to) | With the fees, to the creator (the fee payout address), or into the pool. | "FEE_HANDLING" With the fees \| "CREATOR" To the creator \| "POOL_DEPTH" Into the pool | FEE_HANDLING |

### Creator-fee cap · `"creator-fee-cap"` (id 5)

At most a fixed amount of ETH per 30 days goes to the creator; the rest is burned or goes into the pool.

- **Available in:** version 2.
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`, `TOKEN_ONLY`.
- **Needs:** A cap on creator fees needs a creator option on at least one fee side.
- **Needs:** The cap is a fixed amount of ETH: it needs native ETH as the pricing asset.
- **Tell the creator:** Optional, only with a creator option on at least one fee side; the launch flow shows it under Box kind (Fee cap). Presets: 1, 4, 8, 16 or 32 ETH per 30 days (32 ETH is the platform's own cap). Shows buyers the creator cannot take unlimited fees.

| Setting | What | Allowed | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `capPerEpoch` (Cap per 30 days) | A fixed amount per 30 days: 1, 4, 8, 16 or 32 ETH, written like { "decimal": "4" }. | 1 \| 4 \| 8 \| 16 \| 32 ETH (write { "decimal": "1" }) | none |
| `excess` (Above the cap) | Burned or into the box's pool (to holders: Demo only for now). | "BURN" Burned \| "POOL_DEPTH" Into the pool | BURN |

### Collection page (banner, teasers, link) · `"collection-metadata"` (id 6)

A banner, up to 4 teaser pictures (labelled 'Teaser' until the reveal) and a website link on the collection page.

- **Available in:** version 1 and 2.
- **Box types:** `NEW_COLLECTION`.
- **Tell the creator:** Optional, new collections only: the launch flow takes a banner (wide, shown at 4:1) and up to 4 teasers, each labelled 'Teaser' and never an item (with hidden art not one of the item pictures), under Contents (Collection page). Its link starts as the Website link from Basics; the creator may change or clear it there. Good for hidden art: it shows the vibe without revealing items. Set at creation only. In a draft write the banner and teasers as "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW"; leave the add-on out when banner, teasers and link are all empty.

| Setting | What | Allowed | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bannerSha256` (Banner) | The banner picture's fingerprint (the launch flow fills it). | "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (the launch flow computes it from the upload), or "0x0" for none | none |
| `teaserSha256s` (Teasers) | Up to 4 teaser pictures by fingerprint (the launch flow fills them). | a list of up to 4 "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW" (one per upload), or [] for none | none |
| `externalLink` (Website link) | An https:// link, up to 128 characters (empty = none). | text, 0 to 128 characters (an https:// link without spaces or quotes) | the Website link from Basics (when it is a valid link) |

## 5. Raise

### 5.1 Raise target · `raise`

How much the curve must collect, in the pricing asset: the same for every box (4.206980085 ETH). When it is reached the box graduates at once and the raise becomes the box's own pool (the creator does not receive it); if it is not reached by the deadline, the box fails and every token sells back for the same amount of the pricing asset from the refund range, which is not necessarily what each buyer paid.

- **Set by:** the version: every box raises the same amount.
- **Allowed now:** 4.206980085 ETH (every box; set by the version).
- **Default:** 4.206980085 ETH (the one raise).
- **Tell the creator:** Nothing to choose: every box raises 4.206980085 ETH, so the curve, the start price and the price at graduation are the same for every box. Show it as a fact.

### 5.2 Deadline · `deadlineDays`

Days after the open by which the raise must be reached. If it is not, the box fails: buying stops and every token sells back for the same amount of the pricing asset from the refund range (what the curve collected, plus the launch tax and held fees). The same amount per token, not what each buyer paid: late buyers paid more.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** 30, 60, 90 or 180 days (0 only in box 0).
- **Default:** 30 days.
- **Tell the creator:** 30, 60, 90 or 180 days. Shorter keeps momentum; longer gives a slow community time but keeps buyers waiting.

## 6. Opening

### 6.1 Opening time · `openAt`

When trading opens: Now (the block after Create), or a clock time in UTC.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** 0 (Now), or a UTC time from Create up to 14 days after it, written "2026-10-06T18:00:00Z" (also read: { "utc": "2026-10-06T18:00:00Z" }, or Unix seconds from 1,788,220,800).
- **Default:** 0 (Now: the block after creation, about 12 seconds).
- **Tell the creator:** Now, the default: trading opens in the block after the creation block, never in it (the creator's own buy goes in first, in the creation block). Or a clock time (UTC) up to 14 days ahead: the window opens with the first block at or after it (on Ethereum up to 12 seconds later; on mainnet a time on a whole minute opens 11 seconds later). If Create lands at or after the time, the box opens in the next block. A draft writes the time as text ending in Z, "2026-10-06T18:00:00Z"; the launch flow's presets (5 min, 1 h, 24 h) and "Pick a time" become a time when Create is pressed. The first 6.4 minutes after the open (32 steps of 12 seconds) carry a falling launch tax (93.2% down to 8.1%) that stays in the box, so the community should not rush in.

### 6.2 Creator's ETH at start · `creatorBuy`

An optional first buy by the creator inside the creation transaction, before anyone else and without the launch tax. Shown on the box page.

- **Set by:** the creator.
- **Allowed now:** 0, or any amount below the raise, e.g. { "decimal": "0.05" }.
- **Default:** 0 (none).
- **Tell the creator:** Must be below the raise. It skips the launch tax that buyers pay at the open and is shown on the box page. Small amounts buy a large share at the start of the curve (at the one raise, 0.05 ETH is about 3.4% of all tokens and 0.1 ETH about 6.7%; about 0.073 ETH is 5%): show the share before they decide and recommend at most about 5%.

## Fixed for every box (never asked)

- **Supply:** 1,000,000,000 tokens per box, about 80% of them on the curve.
- **Curve:** the price rises about 14.7 times from the first buy to the last (a span of 26,880 ticks).
- **Fee:** 1% on every trade. Of each fee: 10/15 as the creator chose (the fee kinds), 3/15 to FIT buy-and-burn, 2/15 into the box's own pool (paired and locked, owned by the box).
- **Launch window:** the first 6.4 minutes after the open (32 steps of 12 seconds) carry a falling tax from 93.1974% to 8.0565%; in every box it stays in the box: its pool after graduation, or the refund range if the box fails (the Misfits have no refund range, so theirs waits until they graduate).
- **Creation:** 0.0015 ETH bootstrap buy (burned) plus gas (estimate: about 4-5 million gas plus about 200 gas per byte of box data such as the trait table; the Overview page shows it) and the creator's ETH at start, from the creator's wallet; at most 8 add-ons.
- **Items out and back (after graduation):** at launch one item is 1/n of all tokens; as burns shrink the supply it takes fewer (the tokens in circulation divided by the items still in the box, as in the Misfits' vault). Hidden art: a random draw for a 1% draw fee, and an item that was returned picked by name for 25% more. Open art: no draw (nothing is hidden); any item still in the box picked by its number for 25% more. 5% off when an item is returned. Fees burned.
- **Pool after graduation:** the box's own Uniswap v4 pool; the box owns its liquidity, nobody can withdraw it and nobody else can add any (ref. F49 D3, F72 (B)).
- **Failure:** a box that misses its deadline stops buying and every token sells back for the same amount from the refund range (the curve's collection, the launch tax and held fees), not what each buyer paid (ref. launch pipeline § 3.7).
- **Fixed forever once created:** name, ticker, logo, description and every value above (ref. F23 C2, F72 (2)).
