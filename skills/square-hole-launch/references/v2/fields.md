## `BoxType`

BoxSpec field 2: what is in the box (box-spec-v2-draft.md § 1-2; format 1's values kept, 4 and 5 added). `BoxSpec.boxType` carries it as a uint8. The member names are the ticket's and the draft's exact names (`SHOP`, `PILE`: core-v2-m1-tickets.md § 2 M1-00; `NEW_COLLECTION`, `EXISTING`, `TOKEN_ONLY`: box-spec-v2-draft.md § 2 field 2), as BoxSpecV1's `BOX_TYPE_*` constants spell them (integrator, M1-00).

| Value | Name | Settings | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `"NEW_COLLECTION"` | none | A new collection in the supply-share vault. Offered in M1. |
| 2 | `"EXISTING"` | none | Existing NFTs (M3); NOT_OFFERED in M1. |
| 3 | `"TOKEN_ONLY"` | none | Just the token. Offered in M1. |
| 4 | `"SHOP"` | none | A shop vault (M2); NOT_OFFERED in M1. |
| 5 | `"PILE"` | none | A coin pile (M3); NOT_OFFERED in M1. |
