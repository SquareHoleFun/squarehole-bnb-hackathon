## Briefs and differences

### Gas Guzzlers · [gas-guzzlers.json](gas-guzzlers.json)

Demo example 1. A creator box that nods to the 2021 gas wars: 100 toy fuel cans, open art. The creator takes the fees as collected (ETH from buys, GUZZ from sells) and the royalties, and puts 0.1 ETH in at the start. The one raise every box has, short deadline.

- Departs from the advice on purpose: the creator buy of 0.1 ETH is about 6.7% of all GUZZ, above the advice of at most about 5%: it is here to show the large-share note. At the one raise every box has, about 0.073 ETH is 5%.
- Departs from the advice on purpose: royalties are 5% to the creator, the highest preset, to show the add-on; the advice for an artist who wants direct income is 2.5% to the creator, and most marketplaces pay none.

### Ultrasound Owls · [ultrasound-owls.json](ultrasound-owls.json)

Demo example 2. A burn box that nods to 'ultrasound money' and the EIP-1559 burn: 420 owls, hidden until drawn. Buyers' fees buy HOOT back and burn it, sellers' fees burn HOOT. Royalties go with the fees. No creator buy. The one raise, 60 days, opens at a set time (UTC) a day or so after creation to build up the reveal.

- An own hidden picture (an owl face) waits for an SVG upload in the launch flow; until then tickets show its neutral picture ("hiddenSvg": "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW").

### Staking Snails · [staking-snails.json](staking-snails.json)

Demo example 3. Meant as a reward box that pays holders in ETH, nodding to slow withdrawal queues: 1,000 snails, open art, the one raise over 90 days, royalties into the pool and 0.2 ETH from the creator at the start.

- Holder payouts (pay holders in ETH: "qStream": "PAY_HOLDERS_Q", "tStream": "HOLDERS_IN_Q") are Demo only for now, marked pending legal review (check that version with --network demo); on testnet once they are built (ref. slice S14), on mainnet only after the legal review. This draft burns SLOW on both sides, the nearest choice everywhere: it helps holders by shrinking the supply instead of paying them.
- Departs from the advice on purpose: the creator buy of 0.2 ETH is about 12.6% of all SLOW, above the advice of at most about 5% (about 0.073 ETH at the one raise every box has): it keeps the example set's figure.
- Departs from the advice on purpose: royalties are 1% into the pool, to show that choice; the advice is off, or 2.5% with the fees.

### gm Coin · [gm-coin.json](gm-coin.json)

Demo example 4. A plain token box for 'gm' culture: nothing inside, holders paid in GM on both sides (the buyers' side buys GM in limited steps). The one raise (4.206980085 ETH), 30 days.

- Holder payouts in GM ("qStream": "HOLDERS_IN_T", "tStream": "PAY_HOLDERS_T") are Demo only for now, marked pending legal review (--network demo); on testnet once they are built, on mainnet only after the legal review. This draft burns GM on both sides, which helps every holder by shrinking the supply, and the creator takes nothing.

### Stable Stables · [stable-stables.json](stable-stables.json)

Demo example 5. Stablecoins, 'stable' as in horses: 250 horses, open art, priced in USDC. The creator is paid all in USDC (the sellers' side sells NEIGH in small steps, with the sell warning) and caps their fees at about 2,500 USDC a month, the rest burned. The longest deadline.

- USDC (6 decimals) is not on the curated list yet, and its raise and cap amounts (the 'ETH equivalent') are not decided; priced in ETH until it is. The cap is a fixed amount of ETH per 30 days: about 2,500 USDC a month is about 1 ETH at $2,500 per ETH, the smallest preset.

### Square Pegs · [square-pegs.json](square-pegs.json)

Demo example 6. Square Hole's own joke, a square peg in a round hole: 69 pegs, hidden until drawn, with 3 special Round Pegs. Meant to be priced in FIT so the buyers' side burns FIT. Opens 5 minutes after creation.

- Priced in FIT ("q": FIT, "qStream": "BURN_Q") needs FIT on the curated list, once FIT trades (ref. slice S12). Until then it is priced in ETH and the buyers' side uses "ALL_TO_FIT" (Burn FIT), which still burns FIT.
- An own hidden picture (a peg) waits for an SVG upload in the launch flow; until then tickets show its neutral picture ("hiddenSvg": "FROM_LAUNCH_FLOW").
- 5 minutes after creation is a launch-flow choice ("5 min", turned into a time at Create); a draft holds a UTC time or 0, so it says Now ("openAt": 0).
