# How the Link Marines version 2 art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG
with `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`.
Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

The code started as a copy of version 1's source and keeps its pen, its stencil alphabet and its way of building a
marine. Everything that makes mess night is new: the walls, the caps, the jackets, the table, the plates, the props
and the scenes. Version 1's folder is left exactly as it was.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The twelve brand colors, the fixed shades used inside the art, and the two cloth blues. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: shared definitions, shapes, paths for circles and rounded rectangles, placing and number rounding |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js` and turns an SVG into PNG bytes |
| `src/type.mjs` | The stencil alphabet and the lettering that draws it, with a record of where every word lands and the guards on what it may write |
| `src/pen.mjs` | The pen: filled shapes with an outline, thick strokes with an ink edge, halftone dots, clipping and the one plaid |
| `src/poster.mjs` | The ground of every picture: the mess hall's panelled wall in four inks |
| `src/face.mjs` | Faces: the head, ears, neck, eyes, brows, nose, mouths, mustaches, stubble, sideburns, freckles, spectacles and braids |
| `src/gear.mjs` | Headgear: the white peaked cap in three widths, and the cook's paper cap |
| `src/tools.mjs` | What a marine holds: a sleeve and fist, spoon, fork, teacup, tin mug, hand bell, salt shaker, pepper mill, menu card, water glass, teapot, jug and the cook's ladle |
| `src/marine.mjs` | One marine, head and shoulders, built from a spec: the mess jacket or the cook's flannel shirt, face, cap, napkin, braids and what the hands hold |
| `src/coins.mjs` | The plain gold coin, the plate, the ration on its plate, the cloche and the orange dog tag |
| `src/items.mjs` | The 12 ranks, each at the table with its plate and place card |
| `src/props.mjs` | Story symbols: floor, the table under its cloth, place cards, coin heaps, the tureen, the pool and its depth post, crates, white caps peeking, the raise bar, arrows, signs, the speech bubble, the claim button and pointer, the rules plaque, the padlock, the crossed-out key, the jar and the candles |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The 7 story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner, and the bare walls the check compares them with |

## The look

Mess night: the squad's formal dinner, in a panelled mess hall, drawn like a screen-printed poster. Every shape is a
flat ink with a boot-black outline. Shade is always halftone dots printed in a second ink, never a gradient, and there
are no glows or textures beyond the dots, the panel mouldings, stitches and the cloth's folds. The marines are
caricatures with big round noses, blushing cheeks and small eyes, in white peaked caps and dress-navy mess jackets,
and every one of them sits behind the same plate with the same one gold coin.

- **Outline.** Each piece of art has one outline width: 10 px on the 1024 px marine pictures, 7 px in the scenes,
  8 px on the cover, 6 px on the X banner, 4 to 6 px on the box banner, and 0.62 modules on the mark's patch.
- **Halftone.** Dots on a staggered grid, 11 to 14 px apart in most places, each dot about 0.24 of that spacing in
  radius. Dots go on the right side of every shape. A light flat shape goes on the left.
- **Blue is cloth.** Dress navy and flannel slate appear only on jackets, cap bands, sleeves and the cook's shirt.
  Navy cloth is shaded with slate dots, never with a darker navy. Walls, grounds and letters are never blue, and
  `lettering()` refuses both cloth colors. The pool's water is sage.

## The wall

- **Panels.** Tall panels, 1.25 times as tall as they are wide, in one ink. Each has a sunk moulding inset by 0.14 of
  its width, dark below and right and light above and left, and a thin groove along its top and left edge.
- **Four walls.** Walnut behind the brand pieces and the mark, khaki, crate wood and ration paper for the marine
  pictures in turn, and ration paper for the scenes. The panels are 224 px wide on the marine pictures and the mark,
  200 px in the scenes, and 160 to 168 px on the banners and the cover. The grid is centred on the picture.
- **Nothing radiates.** The wall has no rays, discs, stripes or bursts, so no picture puts a marine in front of
  anything that looks like a flag.
- **The table.** A white cloth covers the table: the top seen from a little above, then the front drop with folds,
  halftone shade on the right and a stitched hem. In the scenes it stands on two crate-wood legs on a wooden floor
  from y 1040. A marine sits behind it, so the cloth hides where the bust ends.

## The stencil alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 10 units tall. Each glyph is one or more square-ended strokes 2.3 units wide, with thin
  bridges 0.7 units wide cut through them, like letters sprayed through a crate stencil. It is version 1's alphabet.
- Letters sit 1.7 units apart and lines 3.4 units apart. Most letters are 6 to 7.6 units wide, M is 8.8, W is 10.4,
  and I is a single stem.
- An optional second print, the same stencil moved down and right, gives titles their shadow: orange under the cap
  white name, boot black under the gold cover line.
- The set is the 26 capitals, the digits 0 to 9, `. , ! ? ' % + - :` and the space.
- `lettering()` never writes the name of the project behind the price coin, writes LINK only inside the box's own
  name, never writes three sevens in a row, and never prints in navy or slate.
- Every block of words records its outside box, so `check.mjs` can keep words 48 px from the edges, and 60 px on the
  X banner.

## The marines

A marine is a bust in a box 1000 units wide and 1000 tall, with the face centred on x 500. `marine()` places that box
anywhere at any size and keeps the outline the same width in the picture.

- **Face.** A spec gives the head's width and height, the jaw and the chin. The brow line sits 38% of the way down the
  head. The eyes sit 64 units under it, low enough to show under a cap's visor, and the nose and mouth hang below.
  Every face is ration paper with blush on the cheeks.
- **Eyes.** Dots, wide, happy, sleepy, squinting or winking.
- **Mouths.** A grin with teeth, a smile, a chew, an O, a call and a cheer with the tongue showing. No mouth holds a
  coin: nobody eats at mess night, the rations wait on the plates.
- **Extras.** Puffed cheeks, stubble, sideburns, freckles, a bushy or a curled mustache, bushy brows, round spectacles,
  one or two braids and a napkin tucked into the collar.
- **The cap.** A white peaked cap with a dress-navy band, a black visor with a shine, and a plain gold coin as its
  badge. Officers' caps are wider and carry a khaki cord. The General's is the widest, with a khaki edge on the
  visor. No cap carries an emblem, an insignia, a rank badge or braid.
- **The jacket.** A dress-navy mess jacket with open lapels, a white shirt front with two studs, a black bow tie,
  two steel buttons and plain shoulder straps. No medal, ribbon, chevron or badge.
- **Hands.** A navy sleeve with a white cuff rises from behind the table to a fist that holds one thing from the
  table. Nobody holds food: the coin on the plate is the whole ration.

## The unique edition

The active 420 portraits come from `unique/picture.mjs`, using the same drawing parts and each rank's original
plate. `ranks.mjs` owns the stable rank/squad computation; `unique/deal.mjs` combines it with the fixed seed and
exact counts in `unique/traits.json`. The dealer never calls the active assignment. This keeps building free of
recursion when `assign.mjs` calls the shared `buildUniqueSet` interface in `scripts/kits/unique.mjs`.

Rank, Squad, Face, Eyes, Grooming, Collar, Mouth, Prop and Wall are the complete edition traits. The twelve
pictures listed above remain checked library inputs. Their face/prop combinations do not select the active
portraits. `unique/README.md` documents the full trait counts, real sample ids, generated filenames and checks.
The edition policy is `2d`: review GLBs are verified by the builder but excluded from the active assignment,
settings and public bundle. `assignment.json` records each unique PNG's filename, hash, byte count and full traits.

## Scenes and brand pieces

These are generic fee and rank illustrations. They do not identify a numbered NFT. The labelled sheets in
`unique/` show actual sample ids and their corresponding traits; the model sheet is for review only.

- **Scenes** are 1200 by 1200, on the ration-paper panelled wall with 200 px panels and the floor at y 1040.
  Captions and alt text live next to each scene in `src/scenes.mjs`, and `check.mjs` makes sure `storybook.md` uses
  the same words. The Private takes the seat in scene 01, the Lieutenant claims in scene 04, and the cook serves in
  scene 03.
- **The flannel.** The cook in scene 03 wears the kit's one plaid: a navy-and-cream flannel shirt in dress navy,
  flannel slate and ration paper. It is a nod to a community in-joke, and one shirt is all the kit uses. The cook has
  two braids and freckles and is drawn after nobody. `check.mjs` proves the plaid is in that one picture only.
- **The quiet seven.** Seven candles stand on the box banner's table, in seven of the eleven gaps between the
  twelve plates. It is the only seven the art counts out. `check.mjs` counts the candles in every picture.
- **The mark** is built on a module of 1/32 of its square. The khaki patch has a radius of 13 modules, and its
  stitches run 1.1 modules inside the rim. The Private is scaled to 0.86 of the bust box and cut off by the patch's
  circle. `check.mjs` renders the logo and the avatar next to their bare walls and proves that 2 modules of clear
  space stay empty and that the patch sits inside 95% of a round crop.
- **The X banner** keeps x 0 to 420, y 270 to 500 as bare walnut wall for the avatar, with the name and the cover line
  above it and the Corporal and the General at the table on the right, a candle between them. `check.mjs` proves the
  corner pixel by pixel.
- **The cover** puts the name in two lines and the cover line on the left, and the Private at the table with a plate
  and a candle on the right.
- **The box banner** seats all twelve ranks side by side at one long table, from Private to General, each behind the
  same plate.

## Rules for new art

- Start every picture with `ground()` from `src/poster.mjs`, and keep the wall free of rays, discs, stripes and
  bursts.
- Use only the palette. A new shade goes into `src/palette.mjs`, and `check.mjs` refuses colors from anywhere else, any
  vivid blue, any color within ΔE 8 of a fenced color, and any blue that is not one of the two cloth colors.
- Draw every letter from the stencil alphabet. Keep words in pictures short, never label a coin, and never write the
  name of the project behind the price coin.
- One marine per portrait, head and shoulders, in the same bust box, behind the same plate, so the collection
  reads as one set. Never give a higher rank more of anything.
- Keep every face ration paper. Tell marines apart by hair, faces and what they hold.
- No weapons, no food piled up, no real emblems, insignia, rank badges, flags, unit patches or camouflage, no real
  people, no mascots or known characters, no coin logos, hexagons, cubes or chains.
- Keep one flannel shirt and one seven. A second plaid or a second count of seven fails the check.
- Keep the twelve ranks in `ranks.mjs` and the base library in `src/items.mjs` stable. Every squad retains one of
  each rank, and every rank appears 35 times.
- The active edition uses the fixed unique seed, full traits and reviewed manifest. A visual change requires its
  own art review; adoption does not change the seed, samples, sheets or manifest.

## Rebuild

```sh
export PATH="$HOME/.nvm/versions/node/v24.22.1/bin:$PATH"
node examples/boxes/link-marines-v2/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/link-marines-v2/assign.mjs > examples/boxes/link-marines-v2/assignment.json
node examples/boxes/link-marines-v2/check.mjs
node --test examples/boxes/link-marines-v2/unique/unique.test.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. Full unique output stays in ignored scratch; `unique/README.md` gives
the direct build commands. The parent check verifies the active edition as well as every original art check. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal
dependency, then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this
repository, the base art renderer needs `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Full edition builds and checks run
inside this repository, with the shared kit tooling and Rent Free model utilities available. Another renderer
version may draw slightly different pixels, and the check will say so.
