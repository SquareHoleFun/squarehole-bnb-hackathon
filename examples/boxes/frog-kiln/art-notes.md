# How the Frog Kiln art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG with
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`. Never edit an
SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The brand colors and the fixed glazes, tints and shades used inside the art. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: shared definitions, shapes, smooth curves through points, arches, rounded rectangles, placing, number rounding and a seeded random source |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js`, turns an SVG into PNG bytes, and reads raw pixels for the checks |
| `src/type.mjs` | The loop-tool alphabet, its carved and brushed treatments, and a record of where every word lands |
| `src/pen.mjs` | The pen: outlined shapes, glazed forms with their shade, lines, bands, clipping, iron speckles and arrows |
| `src/studio.mjs` | The ground of the wide pictures: the limewashed wall, the plank floor and bricks in running bond |
| `src/kiln.mjs` | The kiln, cold, glowing or firing, its smoke, and the FIRED counter plaque |
| `src/toad.mjs` | One toad figurine built from a spec, and the toad wrapped in paper |
| `src/coins.mjs` | The frog coin, the KILN tile and the ash flake of a burned coin |
| `src/props.mjs` | Story symbols: the crock, the pond, the Square Hole crate, the raise bar, clay tags, the peel, bowls, the dish, the glass jar, the shelf and a sparkle |
| `src/items.mjs` | The glazes, the 12 toad variants and their pictures |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The 7 story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner, the bare grounds the check compares them with, the cover line and the literal line |
| `src/hidden.mjs` | The hidden picture, drawn by hand in whole numbers so it fits the on-chain field |

## The look

A potter's studio. A limewashed bisque wall, a brick kiln, a plank floor, and glazed clay. Every shape is a flat glaze
with a soot outline. Shade is the same shape moved up and left inside itself, so a crescent of a darker glaze shows on
the lower right. Light is a flat shine on the upper left. Some glazes carry iron speckles, small dots scattered by a
seeded random source. There are no gradients, filters or see-through colors, and the check refuses them.

- **Outline.** 11 px on the toad pictures, 7 px in the scenes and half a module on the mark. On the cover and the
  banners the plaque and the kiln use 5 to 7 px and the small toads 4 to 6.5 px. Small things in a scene use 4 to 6 px.
- **Light.** Always from the upper left, on every shape.
- **Fire.** Flat rings of flame, ember and a pale core, with tongues of flame and a white-hot center. The fire is the
  warmest thing in any picture, and nothing else uses its core color.

## The wall, the floor and the kiln

- **Wall.** Bisque with a few wide, soft sweeps of a slightly darker limewash, placed by a seeded random source for
  each piece. The mark's wall is plain, so its clear space is plain too.
- **Floor.** Wide planks with a lit top edge and seams. Scenes put the floor at y 1040.
- **Bricks.** Running bond, each brick twice as long as it is tall, with light mortar between. About one brick in six is
  lighter and one in eight darker, so the wall of the kiln looks laid by hand.
- **Kiln.** A box 600 units wide and 700 tall. A dome on short walls, a chimney with a lip, a steel strap with five
  rivets, an arched door in a ring of wedge bricks, a spy hole and a footing. It is cold (a dark door with ash), glowing
  (a bed of embers) or firing (three tongues of flame, sparks and smoke from the chimney). The smoke rises up to 170
  units above the box, so leave room over a firing kiln.
- **Counter.** A dark slab plaque with FIRED cut into it, over five number wheels that show no number.

## The loop-tool alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 10 units tall. Each glyph is one or more strokes 1.9 units wide with round ends, like lines cut
  with a potter's loop tool. Most letters are 5.2 to 7.2 units wide, M is 8, W is 9.2, and I is a single stem.
- Letters sit 1.15 units apart and lines 3.6 units apart.
- Each letter leans up to 1.8 degrees and sits up to 0.22 units off the line, by a seeded random source for each text,
  so a word looks cut by hand and every build is the same.
- **Carved.** Three strokes of the same letters. A light rim, 2.15 units wide and moved down and right, where the cut
  edge catches the light. The dark groove, 1.9 units. The clay floor, 1.42 units, moved down and right, so a dark lip
  shows on the upper left of every cut. Used on the name plaque, the clay tags and the counter.
- **Brushed.** One soot stroke 1.75 units wide, like underglaze brushwork. Used for the cover line on the wall, the
  crate's label, the counts in scene 2, and in cobalt for the 420 on the crock.
- The set is the 26 capitals, the digits 0 to 9, `. , ! ? ' : - % +` and the space.
- Every block of words records its outside box, so `check.mjs` can keep words 48 px from the edges, and 60 px on the X
  banner.
- To add a glyph, add `{ w, d }` to the table in `src/type.mjs`: its width in units and its strokes as path data, with
  0 at the cap line and 10 on the baseline, inset by half a stroke.

## The toads

A toad is a figurine in a box 1000 units wide and 1000 tall, centred on x 500 with its foot on y 900. `toad()` places
that box anywhere at any size and keeps the outline the same width in the picture.

- **Body.** One smooth curve through 26 points, with two eye bumps on top, haunches low on the sides and a flat foot. A
  crease on each side marks the haunch.
- **Glaze.** The base color, the shade crescent, a lighter belly, twelve raised warts in a second glaze, a shine
  stroke on the upper left and a small shine on each eye bump.
- **Raw foot.** The glaze stops above the foot on a wavy line. Below it the clay is bare terracotta with a few dark
  specks, and four rounded drips of glaze hang into it. The unglazed toad has no drips.
- **Eyes.** A dark bead in a golden ring, like a toad's eye, with a glint on the upper left. The moods are bead, wide,
  looking up, sleepy (a lid of the body's glaze), closed (a happy arc) and wink.
- **Mouth.** A wide smile, a grin with dimples, or a small O, with two nostrils above. On light glazes the lines are
  soot. On dark glazes they are clay, white or ember, as if cut through the glaze.
- **Front legs.** Short and thick at the shoulder, elbows out, with three round toes each and bare clay at the bottom.
- **Patterns.** Carved rings, the orange blush of a shino glaze, runs of ash glaze, fat cobalt brush spots, or iron
  speckles.
- **Extras.** A tiny pinch pot worn as a hat.
- **Cracks.** A crack runs from the head down to the foot with two branches, clipped to the body. Gold seam: a gold
  band with a light core over a soot edge. Riveted: a soot crack held by three steel staples. Ember: a flame band with a
  white-hot core and three sparks.
- **Wrapped.** The hidden toad is the same shape under a paper skin, with folds, a twist on top and a clay seal with a
  question mark.

## Scenes and brand pieces

- **Scenes** are 1200 by 1200, on the studio wall with the floor at y 1040. Captions and alt text live next to each
  scene in `src/scenes.mjs`, and `check.mjs` makes sure `storybook.md` uses the same words. Scene 1 shows the Gold seam
  toad coming out of the crock, scene 5 four toads on a shelf, and scene 6 the Ash toad at home.
- **The mark** is built on a module of 1/32 of its square. The kiln is 20 modules wide on a footing 1.6 modules tall,
  with a door 9.2 modules wide and the plain celadon toad drawn 11.6 modules wide in it. The kiln sits 0.8 modules lower
  on the grid, so it looks centred in a circle. `check.mjs` renders the logo and the avatar next to their bare walls and
  proves that 2 modules of clear space stay empty and that the kiln sits inside 95% of a round crop.
- **The X banner** keeps x 0 to 420, y 270 to 500 as bare wall and floor for the avatar, with the name plaque and the
  cover line above it, three toads on a shelf in the middle and the firing kiln on the right. `check.mjs` proves the
  corner pixel by pixel.
- **The cover** puts the name in two lines on the plaque and the cover line under it, and the firing kiln, the coins
  tumbling into it, the celadon toad and the counter on the right.
- **The box banner** puts the name and the cover line on the left, and all twelve toads on a two-shelf ware rack on the
  right, in variant order.

## The hidden picture

`src/hidden.mjs` draws the wrapped toad again by hand, on a grid of 240 units with whole numbers only, so the whole
SVG is one line of printable ASCII under 2,000 bytes. A box with hidden art stores this text on chain, where the field
takes at most 4,096 bytes. It uses the same palette and the same look as the rest of the art. The check proves the
size, the characters and that `art/hidden.svg` is exactly that text with no line break added.

## Rules for new art

- Start every wide picture with `wall()` and `floor()` from `src/studio.mjs`, and keep the wall calm behind words and
  the avatar corner.
- Use only the palette. A new glaze or shade goes into `src/palette.mjs`, and `check.mjs` refuses colors from anywhere
  else.
- Cut every letter from the loop-tool alphabet. Keep words in pictures short, and never letter a coin.
- Draw every word with `carved` or `brushed` in `src/type.mjs`, so `--no-words` can leave it out.
- One toad per item picture, in the same box and pose, so the collection reads as one set. New toads differ by glaze,
  eyes, mouth and small pottery details.
- Keep the toads animals, with no lips, eyebrows, human faces, clothes, hands, crowns or catchphrases, and never a known
  frog character, cartoon frog or frog mascot.
- Keep the frog coin a plain green coin and KILN a clay tile.
- To add a variant, add an entry to `SPECS` in `src/items.mjs` and its name, count and traits to `VARIANT_NAMES`,
  `COUNTS` and `TRAITS` in `assign.mjs`, in the same order. The counts must still add up to 420. Then rebuild,
  regenerate `assignment.json` and run the check.
- The assignment depends on the variants and the counts. Fix the set before the collection is created, and only then
  make the real list with the secret seed.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/frog-kiln/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/frog-kiln/assign.mjs > examples/boxes/frog-kiln/assignment.json
node examples/boxes/frog-kiln/check.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal dependency,
then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this repository, install
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Another renderer version may draw slightly different pixels, and the check will
say so.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/frog-kiln/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner and
the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs, `withoutWords` in
`src/type.mjs` makes `carved` and `brushed` draw nothing and put nothing in `DRAWN`, and `measure` still sizes every
block. The name plaque, the clay tags, the crate's label and the kiln counter stay where they are, blank. The name, the
cover line, the 420 on the crock and the numbers go; the studio, the kiln, the toads, the coins, the tiles, the bowls,
the pond and the jar stay. The normal build does not change.
