# How the One Dollar is all you need art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG with
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`. Never edit
an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The fourteen brand colors, the flame's fixed order and the fixed shades. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: shared definitions, ids per picture, shapes, gradients, clips, wedges, number rounding and a fixed random sequence |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js`, turns an SVG into PNG bytes, and reads raw pixels for the checks |
| `src/type.mjs` | The salon alphabet with its dollar sign, and the lettering that draws it and records where every word went |
| `src/bill.mjs` | The dollar: the cartoon bill, its strips, the dashed outline of strips taken, and taken strips on their own |
| `src/coins.mjs` | Plain silver coins face-on and lying flat, rows of them, and the four-pointed glint |
| `src/tokens.mjs` | Tokens with their rosette, face-on and lying flat, rows, and the heap of all the tokens there are |
| `src/gallery.mjs` | The room: wall, wainscot, floor, darker corners, the spotlight and its glow, and the ivory frame |
| `src/exhibit.mjs` | The brass frame with its velvet box and glass, the hook and cord, the velvet rope, the museum label and the whole exhibit |
| `src/props.mjs` | The marble fireplace and its fire, sparks, the fountain pool, the jar, the brass plaque, the envelope, arrows, braces, the raise bar and the lettering helpers |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The seven story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner, the name's two lines, the cover line, and the bare grounds the check compares them with |

## The look

A claret gallery at night, lit by one spotlight, drawn like a page from a museum catalogue. Shapes are flat colors
with soft light and thin ink lines, like an engraving. Light falls from the top left, and the spotlight falls from the
top. One green bill hangs on the red wall, so the dollar is the brightest and the only green thing in the room.

- **Lines.** Each thing's ink line is set by its size: 3.5% of a coin's or token's radius, 0.6% of the bill's width
  for its edge, 0.5% of the bill's width for the frame, and 0.8% of the width of a fireplace or a basin. Lines are
  never thinner than 0.8 px on the bill and 1.1 px elsewhere.
- **The room.** The wall runs from `#2F0C17` at the top through claret to `#3F111E` near the floor. Scenes have a
  floor from y 880 down, with four floorboard seams that open up toward the front, and a wainscot 220 px tall, made
  of a chair rail and moulded panels, dark on their upper left edges and lit on their lower right. Things nearer to us
  stand lower on the page.
- **The spotlight.** A small black lamp and a cone of warm light (`#FFF2D6`) that fades from 10 to 20% opacity at the
  lamp to nothing, softened with a blur, and a round glow behind what it lights. The corners of every piece are a
  little darker.
- **The frame.** An ivory rule 28 px in from the edge on the scenes, 3 px wide at 62%, a second rule 10 px further in,
  half as wide at 30%, and a small ivory square on each corner. The X banner leaves its bottom-left corner out for the
  avatar.

## The salon alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 10 units tall, the cap line at 0 and the baseline at 10. A stem is 1.8 units wide and a
  hairline 0.36, a fifth of a stem. `weight` scales the stems and `hair` sets the hairline as a share of a stem:
  labels use 0.3 so their hairlines still read, and the bill's big 1 uses 0.28.
- Serifs are unbracketed hairlines that reach 1 unit past a stem on each side.
- Bowls are oval rings, as thick as a stem at the sides and a hairline at the top and bottom. The U's bowl joins a
  thick stem to a hairline stem, so its inner oval sits to the right of the outer one.
- S, C, G and most digits are drawn with an oval pen 1.8 units wide and 0.36 tall, which makes upright runs thick and
  level runs thin. Their ends taper, so the C, G and S end in their own thin upright beaks.
- The alphabet has no dollar sign, so no picture can letter one. The ticker is lettered as the digit 1.
- The arms of E, F, L, T and Z end in slim upright wedges. J, 2, 3, 5, 6, 9 and the punctuation end in balls.
- The room between letters is 1.25 units plus 15% of a stem, with small fit corrections for round and slanted sides
  and a short kerning table for pairs such as LA, AV and TA. A space is 3 units. Lines sit 4.2 units apart.
- The set is A to Z, 0 to 9, `. , ' : ! ? - · / + %` and four arrows.
- Labels draw an ink edge 11% of the cap height wide (at least 2 px) under the color. Titles draw a soft ink shadow,
  moved 3% of the cap height right and 5% down at 45%, under ivory. Inside a line, the ticker 1 is set in rose by
  `roseTicker()`.
- To add a character, add a function to the table in `src/type.mjs` that lists its parts in grid units, using `V`
  stems, `H` bars, `D` slanted strokes, `Z` polygons, `O` oval rings, `P` pen curves, `B` balls, `S` serifs and `K`
  beaks. The comment at the top of the file explains each.
- `lettering()` records the box and the words of every line it draws in `DRAWN`. `check.mjs` reads it to keep words
  clear of the edges and to see which words each picture letters. Measuring alone records nothing.

## The dollar

The same dollar appears in the logo, the banners, the cover and every scene. `bill()` in `src/bill.mjs` measures
everything in the bill's width w. The bill is 2.2 times as wide as it is tall, a little stockier than a real note.

- **Paper.** A pale green card (`#DCE8CF`) with corners rounded at 1.8% of w, lit at the top (`#EEF4E4`) and a
  little darker at the bottom (`#BFD3AE`), with an ink edge.
- **Border.** An outer rule 2.8% of w in from the edge and an inner rule at 7%, in deep green. Between them, a band of
  two waves that cross every half wavelength, like a cord, with a small square and a diamond at each corner.
- **Field.** Fine level lines every 1.2% of w at low opacity, like an engraving.
- **Medallion.** A round field 30% of w across in the middle, with three interleaved twelve-petal curves of fine
  lines, and a big salon 1 in deep green with a pale edge, 68% as tall as the medallion.
- **Side panels.** On each side, a small framed panel with ONE in it, 31% of w from the middle.
- **Small bills.** Below 200 px wide, the bill drops the waves, the fine lines, the petals and the side panels, which
  would only blur, and keeps its rules, its medallion and its 1.
- **Strips.** `strips` cuts the bill into equal upright strips, counted from the left, with ink seams. `yours` rings
  strips in ivory and lightens them. `gone` clips strips away and adds an ink edge where they were cut, and
  `stripOutline()` draws a dashed green line where they were. `billStrips()` draws taken strips on their own, moved,
  with a seam between strips taken together.
- **In its frame.** `framedBill()` in `src/exhibit.mjs` lays the bill on a mat of dark red velvet in a deep brass
  frame, a shadow box. Around the bill, the mat is 7.5% of w, the box's depth 3% and the moulding 7%. The moulding
  is lit on the top and the left, with a dotted bead line along its middle. The box's velvet walls are darker on the
  top and the left. The glass has a faint tint, three slanted streaks of light and a glint in its top right corner.
  The frame hangs from a brass hook by a cord, and drops a soft shadow down and to the right.
- **The exhibit.** `exhibit()` hangs the framed bill on the wall with the spotlight from above and a glow behind it,
  and stands the velvet rope on two brass posts on the floor in front.

The bill is our own cartoon. The brand book's homage and IP section lists what it never copies, and `check.mjs`
refuses those words in every picture.

## Scenes and brand pieces

- **Scenes.** 1200 x 1200, the floor at y 880, words at least 48 px from every edge. Each scene's title, alt text and
  caption live in `src/scenes.mjs`. `storybook.md` repeats them word for word, and `check.mjs` compares the two.
  Scenes 02 and 04 show no dollar, so their pictures are the same as in version 1.
- **Logo and avatar.** The framed bill on its hook, on the claret ground with a soft spotlight and glow, on a grid of
  32 modules. The bill is 18.4 modules wide with its middle at 16 and 17.4 modules. The logo's frame is slimmer than
  the scenes' frames: a mat of 5% of the bill's width, a depth of 2.2% and a moulding of 6.2%. The avatar is the same
  mark at 400 px.
- **X banner.** The name in ivory salon capitals on two lines, ONE DOLLAR and the smaller IS ALL YOU NEED, the cover
  line under it with the ticker 1 in rose and the second sentence in green, the framed dollar on the right, and a bare
  bottom-left corner for the avatar. Words stay 60 px from every edge.
- **Cover.** The name across the top, THE TOKEN IS 1 and the fire on the left, the framed dollar in the middle, and
  THE DOLLAR IS IN THE BOX on a museum label to its right.
- **Box banner.** The name and the cover line on the left, the framed dollar in the middle so a narrow crop keeps it,
  and on the right tokens falling from a big heap into the fireplace.

## Rules for new art

- Use only the palette. A flame always runs flame, ember, amber and ivory from the outside in.
- The dollar is always the green bill with its big 1. Plain coins stay plain. Tokens are always rose with their
  rosette, and nothing letters a 1 on a token.
- Draw every letter from the salon alphabet. Keep words short and in capitals. Letter the ticker 1 in rose, never in green.
- Draw every word with `lettering` in `src/type.mjs` (`label`, `title`, `placard`, `plaque` and the bill already do), so
  `--no-words` can leave it out.
- No picture letters a dollar sign. No price, no amount and no other money sign appears in a picture.
- Follow the bill rules in the brand book's homage and IP section. The bill's only words are 1 and ONE.
- No people, hands, faces, mascots, characters, brands or charts. Nothing taped to a wall.
- The price coin's name never appears in a picture.
- Keep the frame on every scene and the floor at the same height.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/one-dollar/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/one-dollar/check.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal dependency,
then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this repository, install
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Another renderer version may draw slightly different pixels, and the check will
say so.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/one-dollar/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner and
the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs, `withoutWords` in
`src/type.mjs` lets `lettering` lay out and measure as always but draw nothing and put nothing in `DRAWN`. Placards and
the brass plaque stay where they are, blank, and the bill keeps its border, medallion and panels with no 1 and no ONE.
The titles, the ticker, the numbers and the labels go; the gallery, the frames, the fireplace, the basin, the jar, the
coins, the tokens and the heap stay. The normal build does not change.
