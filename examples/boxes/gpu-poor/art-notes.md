# How the GPU Poor art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG
with `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`.
Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The eleven brand colors, the heat ramp in its fixed order, and the fixed art mixes. No other file defines a color |
| `src/type.mjs` | The tube alphabet, the code that places and layers its strokes, and the recorder that lists the words a picture draws |
| `src/heat.mjs` | Flames, fires, heat glows, contour rings, heat discs and the cold ember |
| `src/props.mjs` | The story symbols (the $NVDA stock coin, VRAM chip, pool, jar, grate and fire, arrows, braces, plates) and the heat camera frame |
| `src/gpu.mjs` | The GPU card, the empty dashed GPU, the slot, and the parts rule for the later GPUs |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The seven story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover, box banner with the named fee loop, and the preview sheet of later GPUs |
| `src/doc.mjs`, `src/render.mjs` | The small SVG builder and the PNG renderer |

## The heat camera

Every picture is the world seen through a heat camera.

- **Cold** things are ice, such as the stock coin, $NVDA, the ice crystals and the cold end of the scale.
- **Warm** things are stacks of bands, cool outside and hot inside, always in ramp order, from violet through
  magenta, orange and amber to white-hot. A thing that is only a little warm stops early in the ramp.
- **Hot air** shows as faint violet contour rings around a fire, each ring a little fainter than the one inside it.
- **The frame.** Four corner marks in violet at 55%, and on the right a heat scale from violet at the bottom to
  white-hot at the top, with a small flame above it and an ice crystal below it. A white pointer shows how warm the
  scene is. In the cold scene it sits at the bottom. The X banner leaves the bottom-left corner mark out for the
  avatar, and the preview sheet has no scale.
- **The ground.** Night, with a soft violet haze. Scenes add a wall that darkens toward the floor, a darker floor
  and a thin rim line where they meet.

## The tube alphabet

- Letters are single strokes on a grid 6 units tall, drawn with round ends and round corners, like bent glass tubes.
  Most are 4 units wide. M is 5, W is 5.6, I is a single line, and dots are strokes of no length, which round ends
  turn into dots.
- The gap between letters is the stroke width plus 1.25 units. A space is 1.4 units. Lines sit 3.6 units apart.
- Labels use a stroke 1.1 units wide in one color. The headline lettering draws the same strokes five times, widest
  first. Violet is 3.2 times the stroke at 35% opacity, then magenta 2.3, orange 1.6, amber 1.0 and white-hot 0.45,
  on a stroke of 0.7 units. That is the hot tube look.
- The set is A to Z, 0 to 9, `% + - = . , : ! ? ' / # $` and four arrows. The dollar sign is there for $NVDA. It
  is a smaller S, 4.5 units tall and 3.6 wide, with one bar through the full 6 units.
- To add a character, add `[width, path]` to the table in `src/type.mjs`. Paths use only `M`, `L`, `A`, `Q` and
  `Z`, with numbers in grid units, the top of the caps at 0 and the baseline at 6.

## The mark

- A square of 32 modules on night. The chip is 16 by 16 modules, corners rounded at 1.8, in the chip shade with a
  rim line, and a pin-one dot in its top-left corner.
- Four pins on each side, 1.3 by 2.6 modules, 3.5 modules apart, in violet.
- The flame is 9.6 modules wide and 11.8 tall, its base 5.6 modules below the centre. It has five bands. Each band
  inside is smaller by 17% of the full flame and sits 0.35 modules higher.
- Behind the chip, a magenta glow and three faint contour rings.

## The stock's name

Version 2 names the stock in the art, as the founder decided. It is always $NVDA, drawn in the tube alphabet.

- **On the coin.** `stockCoin` letters $NVDA when the coin's radius is `TICKER_COIN_R`, 80 px, or more. The building
  shrinks to 60% and moves up by 0.2 of the radius. The letters start 0.11 of the radius below the centre, 0.23 of
  the radius tall, in deep ice `#174E70` on ice, 5.47:1. A post often shows a 1200 px scene 500 px wide, and letters
  on a coin of radius 80 px are still 7.7 px tall there. Smaller coins keep the full building and no letters. Today
  only the big fee coin in scene 02 is named this way. Its letters are 18.9 px tall, 7.9 px in a post.
- **Under small coins and chips.** Where a scene must name a small coin, the name goes under it, the way the fee loop
  does it. Scenes 01 and 05 write $NVDA in ice under their trade coins, and scene 01 writes VRAM in orange under its
  chip. The labels are `NAME_LABEL`, 28 px tall, as tall as the 1% labels, so 11.7 px in a post.
- **Under the fee loop.** On the cover, the X banner and the box banner the loop names its two coins, $NVDA in ice
  under the stock coins and VRAM in orange under the chips, 102 loop units below its middle. Their caps are 28 px on
  the cover, 26 px on the X banner and 30 px on the box banner, and every letter stays at least 60 px from the
  edges.
- **Never in a picture.** A picture never shows the words Nvidia, Ondo or NVDAon, Nvidia's logo or any part of it,
  Ondo's logo, any green, Nvidia's product names or its CEO. The mark is the VRAM chip and carries no letters.
- **The check.** `recordLettering` in `src/type.mjs` lists every word a picture draws, with its size if asked.
  `check.mjs` runs each piece through it, scans the words with the same rules as the docs, and fails if a picture
  letters Nvidia, Ondo or NVDAon or if the mark letters anything. It also fails if a word in a scene or on the cover
  is under 7.5 px tall when a post shows the picture 500 px wide.

## The GPU card

Built on a module u. Every card is 11 modules tall.

- **Length.** 17 modules with one fan, 28 with two, 39 with three, and 28 for a blower card.
- **Fans.** Radius 4.7 modules, 10.7 modules apart, centred on the card. Each fan is a dark opening, heat bands for
  its heat level, seven curved blades over the bands, and a hub with a small hot dot. A blower card has a closed
  shroud with six grille slots and one smaller fan at the far end.
- **Shroud.** Rounded at 1.6 modules, in dark (panel), steel or white (paper), or bare, where there is no shroud and
  the fins show. An accent stripe runs along the top and a shorter one along the bottom.
- **Card parts.** A steel bracket on the left end with four vent slots, a dark board edge, 17 gold contacts in amber
  below it, and a power plug on the top edge.
- **Fixes.** Clean, a strip of torn tape across the card, two white zip ties around it, or a missing last fan, an
  empty hole with two strips of tape in an X.
- **Empty.** The dashed outline uses dashes of 1.4 modules and gaps of 1.1, in violet at 70%, over an empty slot.

## The later GPUs

A later step can add GPU items in a box of their own (see [details.md](details.md)). `gpuParts(id)` in
`src/gpu.mjs` gives each id from 1 to 1,024 its own mix of five parts.

| Part | Bits of the code | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fans | 0 and 1 | one, two, three, blower |
| Shroud | 2 and 3 | dark, steel, white, bare |
| Heat | 4 and 5 | 1, 2, 3, 4 bands past violet |
| Fix | 6 and 7 | clean, tape, zip ties, missing fan |
| Accent | 8 and 9 | ice, violet, magenta, amber |

- **The shuffle.** `gpuCode(id)` takes id minus 1, multiplies it by 717, adds 321 and keeps the remainder after
  dividing by 1,024. Then it mixes the top bits into the bottom ones, by taking the number XOR the same number
  shifted right by 5. Every step can be undone, so 1,024 ids give 1,024 different codes, and neighbouring ids
  look different.
- **No rarity.** Across ids 1 to 1,024 every mix appears once and every choice exactly 256 times. With the
  registry's test size of 100 GPUs, ids 1 to 100, every mix still appears at most once and each choice 20 to 31
  times.
- **The preview.** `art/later/gpu-preview.png` shows ids 1, 92, 144, 224, 265, 533, 624, 676, 737, 779, 857 and 910.
  They were picked so that every choice of every part shows exactly three times. `check.mjs` checks the rule and
  the preview.
- **For the later kit.** Draw one picture per id with `gpu(doc, { ...gpuParts(id) })` on the same night ground and
  frame, and keep the rule fixed before the collection is created. Changing the rule changes almost every GPU's
  picture. Changing the count does not, because each id's parts depend on the id alone, as long as ids stay within
  1 to 1,024. The rule is a starting point. The later kit may also pick a small set of pictures instead,
  as the other example kits do.

## Rules for new art

- Draw every letter from the tube alphabet. Keep words in pictures short.
- Draw every word with `lettering` in `src/type.mjs` (`label`, `plate` and `stockCoin` already do), so `--no-words`
  can leave it out.
- Use only the palette. Warm things follow the ramp order, cold things are ice, and there is no green.
- No people, mascots, characters or faces, no coin logos, no brands, no real card designs, model names or numbers,
  and no charts. The one real name is $NVDA, in the tube alphabet.
- A picture names the stock only as $NVDA. It never letters Nvidia, Ondo or NVDAon, and never draws Nvidia's or
  Ondo's logo or any part of them.
- Keep the heat camera frame on every scene, and show how warm the scene is on its scale.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/gpu-poor/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/gpu-poor/check.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal
dependency, then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this
repository, install `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Another renderer version may draw slightly different
pixels, and the check will say so.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/gpu-poor/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner and
the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs, `withoutWords` in
`src/type.mjs` lets `lettering` measure its box as always but draw and record nothing. Plates stay where they are,
blank, and a big stock coin keeps its building small and high, with no name under it. The names, the headline, the
numbers and the labels go; the coins, chips, fires, pools, jars and the heat camera frame stay. The normal build does
not change.
