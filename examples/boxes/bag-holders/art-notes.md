# How the Bag Holders art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG
with `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`.
Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

The active NFT edition is built in `unique/`: 420 distinct pictures and 420 3D models from `kind`, `trim`,
`repair`, `tag` and `stitch`. `assign.mjs` consumes the shared `buildUniqueSet` result and records the complete
traits and both file hashes. The twelve pictures in `art/items/` are base family inputs. Keep their reviewed
bytes, the fixed seed and the unique manifest and samples stable during adoption.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The ten brand colors and the fixed shades used inside the art. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: shared definitions, shapes, rounded rectangles, number rounding |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js` and turns an SVG into PNG bytes |
| `src/type.mjs` | The sticker alphabet and the lettering that draws it |
| `src/pen.mjs` | The pen: filled shapes with an ink outline, thick strokes with an ink edge, and silhouettes for shadows |
| `src/wall.mjs` | The pegboard and its hole grid, the shelf, and the steel hook |
| `src/bags.mjs` | The twelve kinds of bag |
| `src/hang.mjs` | One bag on one hook: its shadow, the hook, the bag, and the hook's curl over the strap |
| `src/props.mjs` | Story symbols: coins, TOTE chips, arrows, brackets, the pool, the box, the jar, the raise bar, buttons |
| `src/items.mjs` | The 12 base family pictures used by the unique generator |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The 7 story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner, and the bare walls the check compares them with |

## The look

A shop wall of plum pegboard. Every bag hangs from a steel hook, and every hook sits in one of the wall's holes.
Every shape is a flat color with an ink outline, like a printed sticker, and everything that hangs throws one flat
shadow down and to the right on the wall. There are no gradients, no glows and no textures beyond the pegboard holes,
stitches and the weave of the sandbag.

- **Outline.** Each piece of art has one outline width: 10 px on the 1024 px bag pictures, 7 px in the scenes and on
  the cover, 6 px on the banners, and 0.62 modules on the mark. Small things in a scene use 4 to 6 px.
- **Shadow.** The silhouette of a hanging bag, in the wall-shade color, moved 22 px right and 26 px down at hook
  size 1, scaled with the hook. The mark uses 14 and 16, so it fits a round crop.
- **Shading.** Each bag color has one darker shade and one lighter shade, listed in `src/bags.mjs`. Shades go on the
  right side and in folds. Lights go on the left.

## The pegboard and the hooks

- **Holes.** A grid of round holes, 64 px apart on the bag pictures, 60 px in the scenes, 50 px on the X banner and 4
  modules on the mark. Each hole has a dark center and a lighter lip below it.
- **Hooks.** A J-shaped steel hook: a collar in the hole, a shank straight down, and a curl that opens upward. At
  size 1 the shank is 76 px long, the curl's radius is 20 px and the rod is 14 px thick. A bag hangs one curl radius
  to the right of its hole.
- **Hooks sit in holes.** `hangBag` in `src/hang.mjs` takes the wall's grid and refuses to draw a hook that is not in
  a hole. Its error names the nearest hole, so a new layout is easy to fix.
- **The shelf.** Scenes, the cover and the box banner have a wooden shelf along the bottom. The bag pictures have
  none, so the bag has the whole wall.

## The sticker alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 6 units tall. Each glyph is one stroke path, drawn three times: an optional colored shadow
  0.9 units down and right, an ink outline, and the fill.
- The stroke is 1.5 units wide. The outline adds 0.55 units on each side. Neighbouring letters sit 0.5 units apart
  stroke to stroke, so their outlines merge into one sticker.
- Most letters are 4 units wide, M is 5, W is 5.4, and I is a single stem. Lines sit 2.4 units apart.
- The set is the 26 capitals, the digits 0 to 9, `% . , ! ? ' + - #` and four arrows.
- `lettering()` refuses the price coin's name, so no picture can write it.
- To add a letter, add `[width, path]` to the table in `src/type.mjs`. The path uses M, L, H, V, A and C in units,
  with 0 at the top of the letter and 6 on the baseline.

Each kind is drawn in a box (center x, top, width, height) and hangs from the hook's curl. Straps run straight from
the bag up to the curl and meet in a round point, so every bag looks held.

## Scenes and brand pieces

- **Scenes** are 1200 by 1200, with holes 60 px apart and the shelf at y 1020. Captions and alt text live next to
  each scene in `src/scenes.mjs`, and `check.mjs` makes sure `storybook.md` uses the same words.
- **The mark** is built on a module of 1/32 of its square. The tote is 16 by 13.5 modules, its top 13.5 modules down,
  and the hook's hole 3.4 modules down. `check.mjs` renders the logo and the avatar next to their bare walls and
  proves that 2 modules of clear space stay empty and that the mark sits inside 95% of a round crop.
- **The X banner** keeps x 0 to 420, y 270 to 500 as bare pegboard for the avatar. `check.mjs` proves it the same way.
- **The box banner** hangs all twelve kinds in two staggered rows, six on each side of the name.

These scenes, brand pieces and word-free references show generic bags, not numbered NFTs. The fee story stays
the same. The contact sheet in `unique/` shows real picture IDs 1 to 48 with their traits; the model sheet shows
IDs 1 to 24 beside the matching front and three-quarter model views. Their source is `unique/samples.mjs`,
and the unique tests compare every sample, render and sheet byte with a fresh build.

## Rules for new art

- Hang every bag on a hook in a hole, and pass the wall's grid to `hangBag`.
- Use only the palette. A new shade goes into `src/palette.mjs`, and `check.mjs` refuses colors from anywhere else.
- Draw every letter from the sticker alphabet. Keep words in pictures short, and never write the price coin's name.
- Draw every word with `lettering` in `src/type.mjs` (`plate`, `button`, `box` and the bag prints already do), so
  `--no-words` can leave it out.
- One bag per item picture, on the same wall, so the collection reads as one set.
- No people, hands, mascots, characters, coin logos, hexagon coins, brands or copies of real bags.
- The twelve base families and `variantFor` mapping are fixed generator inputs. Adding a kind or changing
  `VARIANT_NAMES` changes the edition and requires a separately reviewed art revision.
- The full trait vocabulary, exact counts and deterministic dealing rules live in [unique/README.md](unique/README.md).
  Preserve their spelling and case in metadata; every Bag Holder must carry both its PNG and GLB.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/bag-holders/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/bag-holders/assign.mjs > examples/boxes/bag-holders/assignment.json
node examples/boxes/bag-holders/check.mjs
node --test examples/boxes/bag-holders/unique/unique.test.mjs
```

Use Node 24.22.1 and the installed workspace. The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first
looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal dependency, then in `packages/cards`, which pins 2.6.2. The active
assignment also requires the repository's shared kit tooling and Rent Free model utilities. Full builds stay
under ignored `.cache/tmp/`; see the [parent README](README.md#rebuild-and-check) for output filenames.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/bag-holders/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner and
the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs, `withoutWords` in
`src/type.mjs` makes `lettering` draw nothing, and `measure` still sizes every block. Plates, the button and the
patches on the boxes stay where they are, blank, and the bags show no print. The titles, labels, numbers and the
sleeping Zs go; the wall, hooks, bags, boxes, coins, chips, arrows and the pool stay. The normal build does not change.
