# How the Exit Liquidity art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG
with `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`.
Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

The active edition is built by `unique/`: 404 distinct 2D pictures with `sign`, `housing`, `route`, `repair`,
`equipment` and `conduit` traits. Its reviewed manifest, fixed seed and samples stay unchanged during adoption.
`assign.mjs` uses the shared `buildUniqueSet` interface and exposes no item models. The generator also makes
GLBs for review; their presence does not change the 2D policy. See [unique/README.md](unique/README.md) for the
complete trait vocabulary, counts, samples and build limits.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The nine brand colors, the fixed art mixes, and the unlit shade of each sign color |
| `src/type.mjs` | The sign alphabet, the heavy logo numerals, and the code that turns cells into shapes |
| `src/sign.mjs` | One sign: housing, face, letters, glow and mount |
| `src/room.mjs` | The hallway: wall, ceiling, floor, doors, hatches, bricks, the night window |
| `src/props.mjs` | Story symbols: coins, EXIT squares, the pool, the crate, the burn, pointer, buttons, arrows |
| `src/items.mjs` | The 12 base sign families used by the unique generator |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The 7 story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner |
| `src/doc.mjs`, `src/render.mjs` | The small SVG builder and the PNG renderer |

## The module

Every size is a multiple of one module, called m. One module is one cell of the alphabet. A sign drawn at
m = 18 and one drawn at m = 7 have the same proportions.

## The sign alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 7 cells tall. Most are 5 cells wide. I and 1 are 3 wide, and the dot, the apostrophe
  and the exclamation mark are 1 wide. A space is 3 wide.
- There is one empty column between letters and three empty rows between lines.
- The set is the capitals we need (no J or V yet), the digits 0 to 5, `? ! . ' % +` and four arrows.
- The heavy numerals are 3 by 5 cells and exist only for 4 and 0. They draw the 404 on the logo and on the
  404 light boxes.
- To add a letter, add 7 strings of `#` and `.` to the table in `src/type.mjs`. All rows of a letter must be
  the same width.

A letter is drawn in one of two ways.

- **Solid.** The lit cells merge into one outline with no seams between cells. Used for light boxes, labels and
  buttons.
- **LED.** One dot per cell with a radius of 0.39 m. Lit dots take the sign color. Every other cell of the
  panel shows an unlit dot in that color's unlit shade. A blurred copy of the lit dots (0.55 m) sits
  underneath as the glow.

## The sign

- **Face.** The letters plus padding. A light box has 2 m at the sides and 1.5 m above and below. An LED panel
  has 2 m all around, so its dots form whole rows.
- **Housing.** The face plus a 0.7 m border with corners rounded at 0.6 m. The outer 0.15 m is rim grey, the
  rest is housing slate.
- **Light box face.** One of the four sign colors with a soft white highlight in the middle and ink letters.
- **LED face.** A black panel, `#070A0B`, with dots.
- **Glow on the wall.** An ellipse 0.95 times the housing width and 1.7 times its height, in the sign color,
  fading out from 32% in the middle. Small background signs use 20% to 24%.
- **Mounts.** Rods are two bars at 20% and 80% of the width, hanging from the ceiling. A bracket is one post
  with a plate on the wall. A flag is an arm from the left wall with two drops. A chain hangs from the
  left corner, and that sign can tilt.

## The hallway

- **Wall.** Hallway ink with faint seams, a ceiling band (`#0A0E10`) with a slate edge, and a floor band
  (`#141C20`) with a baseboard and a lighter edge.
- **Item pictures.** 1024 by 1024. Ceiling at y 120, floor at y 880. The sign is centered at x 512. A sign alone
  sits at about y 470. A sign with a door below it sits higher, about y 318 to 360.
- **Scenes.** 1200 by 1200. Ceiling at y 96, floor at y 1056.
- **Doors that lead nowhere.** A doorway filled with bricks, a door the size of a mouse hole, a door painted on
  the wall, a bolted floor hatch, a bolted ceiling hatch, and a whole wall of bricks.

## Rules for new art

- Draw every letter from the sign alphabet. Keep words in pictures short.
- Lay out every word with `blockCells` (signs, `label`, `plate` and `button` already do), so `--no-words` can leave
  it out.
- One sign per item picture, on the same hallway, so the collection reads as one set.
- Use only the palette, and one glow color per sign.
- No people, mascots, characters, coin logos, brands, real safety pictograms or copies of real signs.
- Keep the twelve base families and their order fixed. Changing `ART`, `VARIANT_NAMES`, the trait vocabulary
  or the seed changes the reviewed edition and requires a separate art decision.
- Check generated pictures against `unique/manifest.json`; never replace that manifest to make a changed
  render pass. The parent checker validates the full active edition separately from the base-family library.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/assign.mjs > examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/assignment.json
node examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/check.mjs
node --test examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/assign.test.mjs
node --test examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/unique/unique.test.mjs
```

Use Node 24.22.1. The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal
dependency, then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. Run these commands in this repository
with its installed workspace dependencies. Another renderer version may draw slightly different pixels, and the
check will say so.

The active assignment also needs this repository's shared kit tooling. Its API supplies the fresh generated
directory; its JSON stores relative picture filenames. Full generated SVGs, PNGs and review GLBs stay in ignored
`.cache/tmp/` directories. `node scripts/kits/bundle.mjs exit-liquidity` exports the public 2D items and complete
traits into uncommitted `build/`, omitting the review models.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/exit-liquidity/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner
and the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs,
`withoutWords` in `src/type.mjs` keeps every block of lettering at its size but lights none of its cells. Signs,
counters, plates and buttons stay where they are, blank: a light box glows plain and an LED panel shows only unlit
dots. The flicker dot goes too. The arrow on an EXIT square is the token's symbol and stays, like every diagram arrow.
The normal build does not change.
