# The house on the lake

Seven scenes that show how Lake House works. Edit the words in each scene's two caption lines; the picture line says
what the scene will show once it is drawn. Only the logo is drawn so far.

![Lake House. A small wooden house on posts at the edge of a lake at night, under a moon, with pines behind it. Its square window glows like a square hole and shines on the water.](art/logo.png)

## 01 · The house

Picture: the house on the lake at dusk, its square window lit. A label on the water: MOCK HOUSE.

This is a mock house on a quiet lake. Its coin is HOUSE.
The house is made up, and the coins are test coins.

## 02 · The company

Picture: a small safe beside the house, with one key on a hook. A card: LAKE HOUSE CO. (MOCK COMPANY).

Lake House Co. is a Safe, a shared wallet for the house.
Today one person holds its key. The company does not exist yet.

## 03 · The manager

Picture: a small robot with a clipboard at the door, a speech bubble that starts with I AM AN AI.

An AI manager answers the guests, books the cleaner and pays for repairs.
It says it is an AI before anything else.

## 04 · The cap

Picture: the robot at a gate with a meter on it: 300 a week. A hand on a switch: OFF.

The manager can pay at most 300 tUSDC a week, to any address.
The chain enforces the cap. One owner transaction switches it off.

## 05 · Guests pay

Picture: small made-up guests drop coins into the Safe. A tag on the coins: TEST.

Made-up guests pay the Safe for their nights.
One wallet pays for all of them, and we say so.

## 06 · Into the pile

Picture: coins leave the Safe for the box's inbox. They split: ten go to the pile, five go on to the pool, FIT and Square Hole.

What is left after the costs goes into the box.
Ten of every fifteen parts land in the pile. Every trade's fee goes there too.

## 07 · Hand in HOUSE

Picture: a holder drops HOUSE tokens into the box and takes out a slice of the pile.

After graduation, anyone hands in HOUSE for its share of the pile.
Nobody else can move the pile. Not the manager, not the company.
