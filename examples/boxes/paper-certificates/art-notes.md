# How the Paper Certificates art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG with
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`. Never edit an
SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The brand colors, the twelve certificate inks with their mats and tints, and the light and shade of each color. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: definitions and clip paths, rectangles, ellipses, arcs, smooth curves through points, placing, number rounding and a seeded random source |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js`, turns an SVG into PNG bytes, and reads raw pixels for the checks |
| `src/type.mjs` | The engraver's alphabet: letter skeletons, the oval pen, lettering with an optional shadow, and a record of where every word lands |
| `src/engrave.mjs` | The engraver's tools: outlined shapes, lines, perforations, hatching, crosshatching, ruled skies, water, the security tint, guilloche bands, rosettes, scallops and signatures |
| `src/certificate.mjs` | One certificate with its coupon sheet: the frame, the corner rosettes, the number and edition boxes, the vignette, the name, the seal, the signatures and the coupons |
| `src/vignettes.mjs` | The twelve vignettes, one per company |
| `src/items.mjs` | The twelve variants: company, ink, frame, rosettes and vignette, and the picture of each certificate |
| `src/coins.mjs` | The gold coin, the index coin, a coin seen edge-on, and the SCRIP coupon |
| `src/props.mjs` | Story symbols: the room and border of a scene, the safe, envelopes, fire, the stove, smoke, the scale, the collection box, trays, tags, the raise bar, arrows, a burning coupon, the cash box, small certificates, the gilt frame, falling coins and the dish |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The 7 story scenes, with their captions and alt text, for both editions |
| `src/brand.mjs` | The mark, the X banners, the covers and the box banners, the bare grounds the check compares them with |
| `src/edition.mjs` | The two editions: names, cover lines, price coins, colors and folders, and the literal line |
| `src/hidden.mjs` | The hidden picture, drawn by hand in whole numbers so it fits the on-chain field |

## The look

An engraved share certificate. Cream paper, one ink per certificate, and every tone made of lines. Shade is parallel
hatching, deep shade is crosshatching, skies and floors are ruled lines, and frames and rosettes are guilloche, the
interlaced curves of old security printing. Flat colors appear only where a child needs them to tell things apart,
such as gold coins, steel coins, lilac coupons, red seals, the iron of the safe and the stove, and fire. There are no gradients,
filters, masks or see-through colors, and the check refuses them.

- **Outline.** 2.6 px on the vignettes, 2.2 to 3 px on frames and props, 3 px on the scene objects.
- **Hatching.** Lines about 1 px wide, 3 to 6 px apart. Shade falls on the lower right of every object.
- **Fire.** Flat tongues of flame, ember and a pale core, only ever inside the stove or on a burning coupon.

## The engraver's alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 10 units tall. Each glyph is a skeleton of thin lines, with hairline serifs on its stems.
- Each skeleton is drawn with one stroke of an oval pen, three times wider than it is tall and tilted 15 degrees. Up
  and down strokes come out thick, crossbars and serifs thin, and round letters swell on their sides. A stroke like \
  is thicker than a stroke like /, as in an old Roman.
- The trick is a stretched drawing space. The stroke runs in a space stretched along the pen's long axis, and the
  skeleton is squeezed by the inverse first, so the letter keeps its shape and only the pen is oval.
- There are three pen weights. Display is for names (cap 40 px and up), text for lines and tags, and small for the
  smallest words, whose hairlines would vanish with the display pen.
- `shadowed()` sets a second copy of the letters down and right in gold or bond green, under the ink.
- The set is the 26 capitals, the digits 0 to 9, `& . , ' - : ! ? %`, the sign º for Nº and the space.
- Every block of words records its outside box, so `check.mjs` can keep words 48 px from the edges, and 60 px on the X
  banners. Words drawn inside a vignette or a small certificate are moved into picture coordinates first.
- To add a glyph, add `[width, path]` to `SKELETONS` in `src/type.mjs`, with 0 at the cap line and 10 on the baseline,
  using only absolute M, L, H, V, C and Z.

## The guilloche

- **Bands** follow the centre line of a rounded rectangle. Several sine waves of the same wavelength, each shifted in
  phase, cross back and forth over it. A whole number of waves fits the frame, so the band closes without a seam. A
  second, faster wave can ride on top for a finer lace. Frames draw the band in paper color on a solid ink ring, which
  reads as white lacework.
- **Rosettes** are hypotrochoids, the curve a pen draws in a small gear rolling inside a ring. The ring and gear sizes
  are whole numbers, so the curve closes after a known number of turns. Each certificate has its own pair for its
  corners and a lighter one for its coupons.
- **The security tint** is fine wavy lines in a pale ink, each shifted a little from the one above, so together they
  make a moiré of lozenges. It is one small tile repeated as a pattern, and its rows shift by whole fractions of a
  turn, so the tile repeats without a seam.

## The certificates

Each certificate is one sheet of paper, 964 px square, on a mat in the deep shade of its ink.

- **Frame.** An ink band 30 px wide with white-line guilloche, a hairline inside it, and the security tint on the paper
  inside. Four medallions sit on the frame's corners, each with the certificate's rosette.
- **Boxes.** On the left, Nº and the certificate's number in seal red; the kit's own pictures print the specimen number
  0. On the right, ONE OF 1,929.
- **Vignette.** An oval 392 by 248 px in a double ring, holding the company's picture.
- **Name.** The company in two lines of display lettering, then a rule with a diamond and THIS CERTIFIES NOTHING.
- **Seal and signatures.** A red wax seal on the lower left, or a gold foil seal on a blue chip. Two signatures are
  cursive loops drawn from a seeded random source, so each company signs the same way on every build.
- **Coupons.** A perforation, then five numbered coupons with a guilloche border and a rosette each.
- **Blue chips.** Blue ink, a gold seal, and a gold ribbon across the top of the vignette with BLUE CHIP on it. Their
  chips are the only bright blue in the collection.

## Scenes and brand pieces

- **Scenes** are 1200 by 1200, on plain paper in a thin guilloche border, with a desk top from y 1000 down. Captions and
  alt text live next to each scene in `src/scenes.mjs`, and `check.mjs` makes sure `storybook.md` uses the same words.
  The paper edition's scenes are the same drawings with the steel index coin, and their captions differ only in the
  coin.
- **The mark** is built on a module of 1/32 of its square. The seal reaches 13.4 modules from the centre at its 44
  scallops. The dark ring runs from 8.9 to 11.2 modules with white-line lace, the gold middle carries a rosette, and
  the S is 9.1 modules tall. `check.mjs` renders the logo and the avatar next to bare paper and proves that 2 modules
  of clear space stay empty and that the seal sits inside 95% of a round crop.
- **The X banners** put the edition's name across the top in engraved letters with a shadow in the edition's color, the
  cover line under it, and three certificates fanned out on the right with coins and coupons. The bottom-left corner,
  x 0 to 420 and y 270 to 500, is bare paper, and `check.mjs` proves it pixel by pixel.
- **The covers** sit in a full guilloche frame, with the name in two lines on the left, the cover line under it, and
  two fanned certificates with a coin and a coupon on the right.
- **The box banners** put the name and the cover line on the left and all twelve certificates in two rows on the right,
  in variant order.
- The gold edition uses gold for shadows and frames and gold ink for lettering. The paper edition uses bond green.

## The hidden picture

`src/hidden.mjs` draws the sealed envelope again by hand, on a grid of 240 units with whole numbers only, so the whole
SVG is one line of printable ASCII well under 4,096 bytes. A box with hidden art stores this text on chain. It uses the
same palette as the rest of the art. The check proves the size, the characters and that `art/hidden.svg` is exactly
that text with no line break added.

## Rules for new art

- Use only the palette. A new ink or shade goes into `src/palette.mjs`, and `check.mjs` refuses colors from anywhere
  else.
- Draw tone with lines from `src/engrave.mjs`, never with a gradient or a see-through color.
- Draw every letter with the engraver's alphabet. Keep words in pictures short, and never letter a coin or a coupon.
- Draw every word with `letter` or `shadowed` in `src/type.mjs`, so `--no-words` can leave it out.
- One certificate per item picture, with the same sheet, frame and coupon layout, so the collection reads as one set.
  New certificates differ by company, ink, frame, rosettes and vignette.
- Invent every company as a pun on a trading word, and never name or draw a real company, brand, product or exchange.
- Keep the gold coin a gold disc with a bar, the index coin a steel disc with a basket, and SCRIP a lilac coupon. Never
  put a price coin in the fire.
- To add a variant, add an entry to `SPECS` and `COUPON_ROSETTES` in `src/items.mjs`, a vignette in
  `src/vignettes.mjs`, and its name, count and traits to `VARIANT_NAMES`, `COUNTS` and `TRAITS` in `assign.mjs`, in the
  same order. The counts must still add up to 1,929. Then rebuild, regenerate `assignment.json` and run the check.
- The assignment depends on the variants and the counts. Fix the set before the collection is created, and only then
  make the real list with the secret seed.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/paper-certificates/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/paper-certificates/assign.mjs > examples/boxes/paper-certificates/assignment.json
node examples/boxes/paper-certificates/check.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal dependency,
then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this repository, install
`@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Another renderer version may draw slightly different pixels, and the check will
say so.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/paper-certificates/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: each edition's cover,
box banner and seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. The gold edition's go
to `art/reference/`, the paper edition's to `art/reference/paper/`. While it runs, `withoutWords` in `src/type.mjs`
makes `letter` and `shadowed` draw nothing and put nothing in `DRAWN`, and `measure` still sizes every block. The
manila tags, the safe's plate and the certificates stay where they are, blank. The names, the cover lines, the
companies, the tag words, the safe's number and the marks on the seals go; the paper, the guilloche, the vignettes, the
coins, the coupons, the safe, the scale, the stove and the tin box stay. The normal build does not change.
