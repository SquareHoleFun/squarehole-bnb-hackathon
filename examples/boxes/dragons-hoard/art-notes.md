# How the Dragon's Hoard art is built

Every picture in `art/` is generated by the code in `src/`. `build-art.mjs` writes each SVG and renders its PNG
with `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2, with system fonts switched off. Change the source, rebuild, and run `check.mjs`.
Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand, because the check compares them with a fresh build.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/palette.mjs` | The eleven brand colors, the flame's fixed order and the fixed shades. No other file defines a color |
| `src/doc.mjs` | The small SVG builder: shared definitions, ids per picture, shapes, number rounding and a fixed random sequence |
| `src/render.mjs` | Finds `@resvg/resvg-js` and turns an SVG into PNG bytes |
| `src/type.mjs` | The gilt alphabet, and the lettering that draws it and records where every word went |
| `src/coins.mjs` | Coins face-on, lying flat and on their edge, coin stacks, and the heap |
| `src/scales.mjs` | HOARD tokens and the dragon's tail: scale arches, spikes, shade, shine and the spade |
| `src/hoard.mjs` | The hoard, a pile with the tail around it measured in the pile's width, the pile's old outline and the pouch |
| `src/lair.mjs` | The sky, the floor, gold glints, the warm glow behind the pile and the page frame |
| `src/props.mjs` | The pool, the fire and its brazier, sparks, the chest, plates, arrows, braces, the raise bar and the lettering helpers |
| `src/scenes.mjs` | The seven story scenes, with their captions and alt text |
| `src/brand.mjs` | Logo, X avatar, X banner, cover and box banner, and the bare grounds the check compares them with |

## The look

A lair of deep lapis, lit by a pile of coins, drawn like a page from an old ledger of hoards. Every shape is a flat
color with an ink outline. Light falls from the top left.

- **Outlines.** Each thing's outline is set by its size. A coin or a token uses 11% of its radius, the tail 10% of its
  width at the root, the brazier 3.5% of its width, the chest 2.2% and the pool 1.4%.
- **Shine and shade.** Coins and tokens have a shine at the top left. The tail's shine runs along the side that faces
  the light, and its shade along the side that faces away.
- **The ground.** The sky runs from `#1A2763` at the top through lapis night to `#0A1030` at the bottom. Scenes have a
  floor from y 880 down, with a thin lapis edge where it meets the wall. Things nearer to us stand lower on the page.
- **Glints.** Four-pointed gold glints, 3 to 11 px from the middle to a point, at 25% to 80% opacity. A fixed random
  sequence places them, and they are kept away from words. The night scene has fewer, smaller ones.
- **The frame.** Two gold rules, the outer one 28 px in from the edge on the scenes, at 70% and 4 px wide, the inner
  one 11 px further in, at 32% and 2 px. The corners curve inward with a radius of 36 px, and a gold dot sits in each
  corner. The X banner leaves its bottom-left corner out for the avatar.

## The gilt alphabet

- Letters sit on a grid 6 units tall. Strokes are even, with square ends and sharp corners, like letters cut into a
  stone plaque. Sharp corners are cut flat once they reach 2.2 times the stroke.
- Thin serifs finish the strokes. A level serif is 1.7 units long on the cap line or the baseline, and an upright
  beak at the end of an arm is 1.05 units. Serifs are drawn at 58% of the main stroke.
- Most letters are 4 to 4.6 units wide. M is 5.6, W is 6.2 and I is a single stem. Dots are square points.
- The room between letters is the stroke width plus 1.1 units. A space is 1.6 units. Lines sit 3.4 units apart.
- Labels draw one color over an ink edge 0.9 units wider. Gilt titles draw four layers. The ink outline is 0.95
  units wider, a bevel in `#7A4E12` is 0.3 units wider and moved 0.24 units down and right, then comes the gold, and
  last a thin shine in `#FFE6A3`, moved 0.12 units up and left.
- The set is A to Z, 0 to 9, `% ' . , : ! ? - + · /` and four arrows.
- To add a character, add `[width, strokes, serifs]` to the table in `src/type.mjs`. Strokes use only `M`, `L`, `A`
  and `Z`, in grid units, with the cap line at 0 and the baseline at 6. Serifs are tokens: `t` and `b` for a level
  serif on the cap line or the baseline, `d`, `u` and `c` for a beak below, above or across a point.
- `lettering()` records the box of every word it draws in `DRAWN`. `check.mjs` reads it to keep words clear of the
  edges. Measuring alone records nothing.

## The hoard

The same hoard appears in the logo, the banners, the cover and five of the scenes. `hoard()` in `src/hoard.mjs` measures
everything in the pile's width w, from the middle of the pile where it meets the floor.

- **The pile.** A heap 0.625 w tall with a soft round top. Its body is the coins' shaded side. Rows of flat coins
  cover it from the back to the front, the outermost coin of each row hangs over the edge, about one coin in twelve
  stands on its edge, and one coin stands on top. A fixed seed decides every coin's place, its size from 0.84 to 1.12
  of the base radius, its tilt up to 15 degrees either way, and how flat it looks.
- **Coins.** The base radius is 0.082 w. A flat coin is an ellipse with its edge showing below, an inner rim and a
  shine.
- **The tail.** Two cubic curves, from a root hidden behind the pile up over its right shoulder, down past its right
  side and along its front. The points, in units of w across and down from the pile's base, are (0.295, -0.176),
  (0.466, -0.477), (0.75, -0.42), (0.727, -0.114), then (0.71, 0.148), (0.375, 0.295), (-0.011, 0.227). The first 30%
  of the curve is drawn before the pile, so the pile hides the root.
- **Width.** 0.239 w at the root, narrowing to 0.105 w where the spade starts. The spade is 0.216 w long.
- **Scales.** Arches as wide as the local width divided by 1.9, in rows 0.6 of an arch apart, each row shifted by
  half an arch, each arch bulging toward the tip. They are the same arches as on the HOARD tokens.
- **Spikes.** Small triangles on the side that faces the light, one every 1.05 root widths, 0.72 of the local width
  tall, drawn under the tail so only their points show.
- **Shade and shine.** The shade covers up to 40% of the width on the side away from the light and thins to nothing
  where the tail turns, so it never jumps from one edge to the other. A thin shine runs on the lit side.
- **The spade.** Two round shoulders and a point, half of it in the darker jade, with a midrib.
- `flip: true` mirrors the hoard, so the tail sweeps around the left side. `h` changes the pile's height, which the
  night scene uses for a smaller pile.

## Scenes and brand pieces

- **Scenes.** 1200 x 1200, the floor at y 880, words at least 48 px from every edge. Each scene's title, alt text and
  caption live in `src/scenes.mjs`. `storybook.md` repeats them word for word, and `check.mjs` compares the two.
- **Logo and avatar.** The hoard sits on a grid of 32 modules, under a few glints and with a warm glow behind it. The
  pile is 17.6 modules wide, its middle at 14.4 and its base at 23.6. The avatar is the same mark at 400 px.
- **X banner.** The name in gilt letters, the cover line in gold and vermilion, the hoard on the right, and a bare
  bottom-left corner for the avatar. Words stay 60 px from every edge.
- **Cover.** The name across the top, the cover line split left and right, a coin dropping onto the pile and a HOARD
  token dropping into the fire.
- **Box banner.** The fire on the left, the name and the cover line in the middle, the hoard on the right.

## Rules for new art

- Use only the palette. A flame always runs vermilion, ember, gold and vellum from the outside in.
- Coins stay plain. HOARD is always jade with scales.
- Draw every letter from the gilt alphabet. Keep words short and in capitals.
- Draw every word with `lettering` in `src/type.mjs` (`label`, `gilt` and `plate` already do), so `--no-words` can
  leave it out.
- Never draw the dragon's head, face, eyes, wings or claws. Only the tail shows, and it always wears its scales.
- No people, mascots, characters, faces, brands, coin logos or charts.
- The price coin's name never appears in a picture.
- Keep the page frame on every scene and the floor at the same height.

## Rebuild

```sh
node examples/boxes/dragons-hoard/build-art.mjs
node examples/boxes/dragons-hoard/check.mjs
```

The build needs no network and no fonts. `src/render.mjs` first looks for `@resvg/resvg-js` as a normal
dependency, then in the workspace package `packages/cards`, which pins it. If the kit is copied out of this
repository, install `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2 next to it. Another renderer version may draw slightly different
pixels, and the check will say so.

## Word-free references

`node examples/boxes/dragons-hoard/build-art.mjs --no-words` writes only `art/reference/`: the cover, the box banner
and the seven scenes as PNGs with no lettering, as reference pictures of the kit's world. While it runs,
`withoutWords` in `src/type.mjs` lets `lettering` measure as always but draw nothing and put nothing in `DRAWN`. The
stone plate stays where it is, blank. The gilt name, the lines, the numbers and the labels go; the lair, the page
frame, the pile, the tail, the coins, the tokens, the pool, the chest and the fire stay. The normal build does not
change.
