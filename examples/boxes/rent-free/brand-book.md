# Rent Free brand book

## Identity

**Name:** Rent Free. **Ticker:** RENT. **Price coin:** USDC. **In the box:** 500 little houses, a new collection with
open art.

**One-sentence joke:** Five hundred little houses, living Rent Free in your head.

**Cover line, in the art:** "500 little houses. No spare keys."

**Banner line, in the art:** "Little houses. Big door energy."

**The literal line next to them:** "In plain words, two thirds of every buyer's fee is shared by the 500 houses in
USDC, and two thirds of every seller's fee buys RENT and burns it. A house outside the box keeps its share for its
owner, and the share of a house still inside buys RENT and burns it."

**Story hook:** There is a box on Square Hole with 500 little houses inside. Put RENT in the box, and you can take a
house out and give it a spot on the street. Every time someone buys RENT, part of the fee is shared by all 500 houses.
Each house on the street gets its share in USDC, in an envelope that only its owner can open. We call that "rent". A
house still in the box has nobody to give its share to, so its share buys RENT and burns it. Every sale pays a little
"property tax" too, and that buys RENT and burns it as well.

The joke is on the word "rent" itself. These houses are pictures. Nobody lives in them, nobody pays to stay, and the
"rent" is a share of trading fees that stops when trading stops. The joke is never on anyone's home, on the cost of a
place to live, or on the people who buy, sell or hold.

## Voice and tone

We sound like the postman on this street. He is friendly and plain, and a little dry. He knows every house and what
waits in every envelope. He says what happens in the order it happens, and he never guesses what a house or RENT might
sell for. When something can go wrong, he says so plainly and leaves the joke out.

- The joke words are always in quotes, with the plain words next to them. "rent" is a house's share of the buyers'
  fees, in USDC. "property tax" is the sellers' share, which buys RENT and burns it. Nobody collects it.
- RENT in capitals is the token. Rent Free is the name.
- Pictures and numbers come first, words second. No urgency, no countdowns, no prices, and no talk about what a house
  or RENT might fetch.
- A house "gets its share" or "has USDC waiting". Nobody lives in it, and it sends money to nobody.
- In pictures, USDC is a plain sky blue circle and RENT a plain clay hexagon, with no letters, symbols or faces.
- The words on the cover and banners are part of the pictures. Everywhere else we write in sentence case.

Words and shapes we never use: earn, yield, returns, gains, profit, income, passive, investment, dividend, reward,
prize, jackpot, wealth, upside, moon, 100x, APY, ROI, guaranteed, "get in early", "floor price", pump, unlock,
seamless, revolutionary, journey, hodl. For the houses: real estate, realty, property (outside the "property tax"
joke), landlord, tenant, lease, rental, mortgage, deed, equity, portfolio, interest, salary, payday, cash flow,
"pays you", "pays its owner", "get paid", "make money", rare, rarity, worth, valuable. The word rent never appears
without its quotes. Square Hole's own token and collection are never named, and the pun about houses and the square
hole is never made. No dash asides, no emoji decoration, and no prices, dates or wallet addresses in posts or
pictures. No claim that a burn sets a price or helps anyone who holds RENT.
<!-- avoid-list:end -->

Some names never appear at all. They are another token that uses the RENT ticker, the board game with little green
houses and red hotels, and brands that list, share or sell homes. `check.mjs` holds their SHA-256 fingerprints and
refuses them in every doc and script, so the kit never spells them, not even in its own check. The avoid list above is
the only place the words in it may appear.

## Palette

Flat shapes on cream, with thick forest outlines, green walls, butter windows and clay accents. These are the colors
the art was made in. A generated picture drifts a few steps around them, and `check.mjs` proves that every color
really is in the pictures.

In the story, a sky blue circle stands for USDC and a clay hexagon for RENT. The open cardboard box with a square hole
in its side is the box, where houses wait. An envelope with a blue seal is a house's "rent", waiting for its owner. A
fire in a green tray is a burn, and a key stands for a house's owner. They are plain symbols. When a page shows real
balances, it also writes the coin's name in text.

## Typography

Live text uses three free font families under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, all on Google Fonts.

| Use | Family and weights | Fallback stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Titles | Baloo 2, 700 to 800 | `"Baloo 2", ui-rounded, "Trebuchet MS", system-ui, sans-serif` |
| Body and buttons | Plus Jakarta Sans, 400 to 700 | `"Plus Jakarta Sans", system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` |
| Numbers, counts, ids | DM Mono, 400 to 500 | `"DM Mono", ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace` |

Baloo 2 is round and heavy, close to the generated lettering on the cover and the banners. Plus Jakarta Sans is plain
and easy to read at small sizes. The fee pies already use these two, outlined, so a page and the pies match. DM Mono
keeps every digit the same width, so amounts and house numbers line up.

The pictures need no font. Their words are part of the generated images. This kit ships no font files. A site that
uses these fonts self-hosts pinned files with their license notice. The repository already holds Plus Jakarta Sans
for the landing page, in `apps/landing/public/fonts/`. Body text is at least 16 px with a line height of 1.5.

## Logo

The mark is the green cottage, the same house as variant 01. It has a dark green roof, a clay chimney, one butter
window with four panes, a dark door and a short ground line, on cream. The files are `art/logo.png` (1024),
`art/x-avatar.png` (400) and `art/logo-48.png`, which shows that the mark still reads at 48 px.

- **Construction.** One module is 1/32 of the square, 32 px on the 1024 px logo. The house and its ground line are
  about 21 modules wide and 19.5 modules tall, from the bottom of the ground line to the top of the roof. They keep
  more than 5 modules of bare cream on every side and sit inside 89% of a round crop.
- **Clear space.** Keep at least 2 modules free on every side. The square files already hold more, and `check.mjs`
  proves that the outer 2 modules are bare cream.
- **Round crop.** In a round avatar, the whole house sits inside 95% of the circle. `check.mjs` proves this too.
- **Smallest size.** 48 px. Below that, write RENT or Rent Free in live text instead.
- **Keep** the roof, the chimney, the lit window and the door together.
- **Don't** recolor the house, add words or the ticker to the mark, give it a face, hang a sign or a price tag on it,
  stretch, rotate or outline it, or set it on a dark ground without its cream square.

On the X banner, the bottom-left corner (x 0 to 420, y 270 to 500) holds only bare cream, so the avatar can overlap
it. Words and houses stay at least 60 px from every edge of the X banner, and at least 48 px from the edges of the
cover and the box banner. The box page shows its banner as a 4:1 strip. The box banner is 1800 x 600, so the page cuts
75 px off its top and its bottom, and those strips hold only bare cream. `check.mjs` decodes the pictures and proves
all of this. The page also shows the name in live text.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Draw little houses as flat front views, with thick forest outlines, green walls, butter windows and clay accents | Draw real buildings, landmarks, streets, maps or places |
| Keep the houses calm and empty, with lit windows | Draw people, faces, pets or known characters, or make a house look like a face |
| Show USDC as a plain sky blue circle and RENT as a plain clay hexagon | Put a letter, symbol, logo or face on any coin |
| Show "rent" as an envelope that waits for its owner | Show piles of cash, money bags, price tags, sale signs or anything rising in value |
| Write "rent" and "property tax" in quotes, with the plain words next to them | Say a house sends money to anyone, or call it a home someone can live in |
| Say a burn makes the supply smaller and sets no price | Say a burn helps anyone who holds RENT |
| Keep each house on its own short ground line | Draw small plastic game houses, red hotels or a game board |
| Keep the joke on the word "rent" | Make fun of anyone's home, the cost of a place to live, or the people who buy, sell or hold |

## Accessibility

Contrast ratios for the solid colors, by the WCAG 2.2 formula, rounded down:

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest on cream | 11.25:1 | Body text on light |
| Cream on forest | 11.25:1 | Body text on dark |
| Forest on card | 9.00:1 | Text on card |
| Forest on mist | 8.15:1 | Text on mist |
| Forest on butter | 7.68:1 | Text on butter buttons |
| Cream on roof green | 5.65:1 | Text on roof green |
| Forest on sky | 5.39:1 | Text on sky |
| Forest on house green | 5.16:1 | Text on house green |
| Forest on clay | 3.92:1 | Large text only |

The first eight pass WCAG AA for normal text. Forest on clay passes for large text only, so on a page clay carries
headings and labels, never body text. Cream on clay, house green or sky is under 3:1, so cream text never sits on
those grounds. `check.mjs` recomputes every ratio in this table.

- Every story picture has alt text in the storybook. Use the same words wherever the picture appears.
- The cover and the banners have words in the picture. Repeat them as live text on the page.
- Never show inside, outside, burned or claimed by color alone. Say it in words.
- The art is still. If a site animates a coin or an envelope, keep it gentle and turn it off for people who ask for
  reduced motion.
- Amounts, house numbers, the burned total and the contract address are selectable text, never only pictures.
  Controls are at least 44 by 44 px with a visible focus ring, forest on light grounds and cream on roof green.

## Homage and IP

- The name is a nod to the saying that a thought you cannot shake lives Rent Free in your head, and to every street of
  little houses, by idea only. The kit names no real place, builder, brand or game, and it copies no known house,
  street or game piece.
- The houses are made up. They are not drawn from real buildings, and none of them is a landmark.
- The art was made with an image generator from written briefs. The mark came from words alone, and every later
  picture was given the kit's own earlier pictures as its guide. Nothing was traced or downloaded, and no outside
  picture was used. See [art-notes.md](art-notes.md).
- The words on the cover and the banners are part of the generated pictures. They use no font file.
- Square Hole is named as the platform. This kit names no other Square Hole token or collection.
- RENT is also used by other tokens. Live pages always show the chain and the full contract address.
- "Property tax" is a joke. No tax is paid to anyone, and nothing here is tax advice.
- Counsel still has to look at the word "rent", the houses' share of the fees, the "property tax" joke, the ticker,
  the rights to the generated art and the license for publishing this kit. See [details.md](details.md).
